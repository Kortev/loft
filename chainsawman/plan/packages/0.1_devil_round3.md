## Build

1. **The devil fills the end.** Seen from anywhere in the corridor, before and after the arena tears, nothing but
   flesh shows in the opening the devil sits in: no clean wall beside it, no gap above it, no sky. Widen and raise
   the mass to the opening, and grow flesh 1-2 blocks out over the walls, floor and ceiling round it so the edge
   isn't a line. Keep the hit box honest: if the look gets wider, what a player can hit gets wider with it.
2. **The torn arena is a stomach.** Where `Arena` opens the rooms and the roof, the new surfaces are flesh (walls,
   and a dome overhead), not sky and not bare room walls. Blocks or a rendered shell, whichever is cheaper; every
   block still goes back exactly when the fight ends.
3. **The grab.** The fingers close round the player (seen in first person as fingers across the screen's edges, in
   third person as a fist round the body), a short hold, then a fast pull down with the camera, and it lets go on
   the mass. No straight grey pole with an open hand.
4. **Swallowed.** Not a blank screen: close flesh walls that move, a slit of light where the mouth is, and each cut
   opens a lit wound that widens until the player bursts out. It must be plain that cutting is what gets you out.
5. **The film is a played fight.** One first-person take with the HUD on, normal FOV, the script's player really
   acting: walk up the corridor to the devil, hit it and see it hurt, the floor turns, land on a ledge, get
   grabbed, fight the heads on the mass, get swallowed, cut out, the core is offered, cut it, the collapse, the
   loot. Shorten waiting by setting the fight up, not by cutting mid-action. About 40-60 s.
6. **Report what the fight is like.** If making that film shows stretches of 5 s or more with nothing to do, hits
   that don't seem to land, or an ending that comes from nowhere, say so plainly with the times. Don't hide it in
   the edit. That's a finding about the fight, and Fable takes it to kortev.
