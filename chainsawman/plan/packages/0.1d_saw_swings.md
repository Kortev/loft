## What to build (look and sound only: no damage, reach or timing number changes)

First look at what exists (`form/Saw.java` and the form's renderer and first-person code) and film a short
"before" of it, so the difference can be shown.

1. **Seen from outside (third person, and by friends).** Each cut is a real swing: a wide arc with the whole
   arm, the body twisting into it, alternating left and right. Every third or fourth cut in a row is a bigger
   one (both arms crossing, or an overhead chop), and the head saw joins a heavy cut if the form has one. The
   arc follows where the player looks (up, level, down). Between cuts the arms don't snap back to rest: they
   carry through into the next swing. The alternation is synced, so others see the same arm you do.
2. **First person.** Both saw arms cross the screen in turn, low and to the sides so the target stays visible:
   not one vanilla hand bob. The blade blurs along its arc.
3. **The cut itself.** A bright arc trail that hangs for a few ticks along the swing; sparks and blood where it
   bites; a very small camera kick per cut (under 1.5 degrees, none when sneaking), and a rising engine pitch
   through a flurry that drops when you stop. Synthesised sounds only.
4. Keep it readable in a long fight: nothing may cover the middle of the screen for more than 2 ticks.
