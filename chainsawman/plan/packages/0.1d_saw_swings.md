## What to build (look and sound only: no damage, reach or timing number changes)

First look at what exists (`form/Saw.java` and the form's renderer and first-person code) and film a short
"before" of it, so the difference can be shown.

1. **Seen from outside (third person, and by friends).** Each cut is a real swing: a wide arc with the whole
   arm, the body twisting into it, alternating left and right. Every third or fourth cut in a row is a bigger
   one (both arms crossing, or an overhead chop), and the head saw joins a heavy cut if the form has one. The
   arc follows where the player looks (up, level, down). Between cuts the arms don't snap back to rest: they
   carry through into the next swing. The alternation is synced, so others see the same arm you do.
2. **First person.** Both saw arms cross the screen in turn, low and to the sides so the target stays visible:
   not one vanilla hand bob. The blade blurs along its arc.
3. **The cut itself.** A bright arc trail that hangs for a few ticks along the swing; sparks and blood where it
   bites; a very small camera kick per cut (under 1.5 degrees, none when sneaking), and a rising engine pitch
   through a flurry that drops when you stop. Synthesised sounds only.
4. Keep it readable in a long fight: nothing may cover the middle of the screen for more than 2 ticks.

## What was built

Built 2026-11-11 on `saw-swings`. Look and sound only: no damage, reach or timing number changed.

- **One rhythm for server and client** (`form/SwingRhythm.java`): the combo step (0 right arm, 1 left, 2 the big cut every
  3rd in a row, restarting after a pause longer than `Saw.COMBO_GAP`) and the flurry count, used by `Saw.slash` and by
  the slasher's own prediction in `ClientCombat`; the server's step still goes to everyone in `FormEventPayload.SWING`,
  so others see the same arm. `Saw.lastStep/flurry` for the tests. The server's slash sound climbs with the flurry.
- **Third person** (`client/SawPose.java`): a sweep winds the arm up high and out behind the shoulder, cuts across in
  front in a wide arc (tilted by look pitch), and is carried on past; the torso winds away then twists hard into it,
  the other arm swings back, the leg under the cut steps in. The big cut raises both arms overhead and brings them down
  crossing low, body bending, head saw nodding. After a swing the arms carry through (`RECOVER`, 7 ticks) and the next
  swing starts from where they are (`SwingFx.prev`, `ClientCombat.sinceSwing`).
- **First person** (`client/FirstPersonSaws.java`): each arm in turn comes up at its own side and sweeps across the lower
  view and out; the other drops away; the big cut brings both down from the top, crossing low. Comes back, not snapping.
- **Trails** (`client/SawTrails.java`): smoothed (Catmull-Rom), last 5 ticks in the world (2 in your own view), thin as
  they age, a hot additive edge along the tip; only while the blade is actually cutting; the head saw trails in the big cut.
- **The cut** (`client/SwingFx.java`): sparks and blood thrown along the cut's direction where the blade passes each
  thing in front; a view kick of 0.45-1.2 degrees per own cut (none sneaking); three new synthesised sounds
  (`form_slash_air` over every slash, `form_slash_heavy` under the big cut, `form_rev_down` when a flurry of 3+ stops),
  in `tools/gen_chainsaw_sounds.py`.
- **Readability**: lens-blood splats were sized by screen height, which on a tall screen put them over the middle; now by
  the shorter side (`LensBlood.java`).
- **Tests**: `swingsAlternateAndAreEachFightersOwn`, `bigCutComesEveryThirdAndResetsAfterAPause` (FormGameTests).
- **Film**: `-Pselftest=form -Ptarget=swings -Pcapture=true` (`SawSwingsFilm.java`): the test ground with four more mobs
  carrying the row on, the mobs tougher (45 health) so each takes a flurry; ~13 s first person, ~13 s outside.
  `work/form/saw_swings.mp4`, `saw_swings_before.mp4`, `saw_swings_sheet.jpg`, raw takes in `work/form/raw/`.
