# 0.7b Server-proof the hotel (loot, pay, one contract per world)

Branch `hotel-share`, cut from `claude/chainsaw-man` after the 3D devil and 0.7a merged. Hotel code is open again.

## What the player gets

With friends, everyone who fought the Eternity Devil gets its loot and the rescue pay, even if they were dead or down
when it fell. Nobody can grab the whole pile, and a spare account standing in the lobby earns nothing. In a world
with several hotels the devil still gives its contract to one player only. Alone, nothing changes except that the
loot lands in your pack.

## Decided

- A boss pays everyone who fought it: loot into each fighter's inventory, pay to everyone who hurt it, alive or
  not. [kortev, `plan/4d_dying_and_options.md`]
- `oneContractPerDevil` means per world, not per hotel. [kortev, same file]
- Fable's picks: each fighter gets the **whole** drop (3 flesh, 2 blood), not a split, so a friend never costs you
  loot. "Fought" = dealt damage to the devil, its core, or one of its shaft heads or arms during this fight.
- Not in this package: the boss growing with the party, the seal's time limit, saving a fight across a restart
  (all 4.6), and freeing a contract whose holder never comes back (waits on kortev's answer about the heart).

## Facts (code in `chainsawman/src/main/java/io/github/kortev/chainsawman/hotel/`; read before you change)

- `Hotel.dropLoot(world, devil)` (about line 985) drops one glowing pile: `FLESH_DROP = 3` Eternity flesh and
  `BLOOD_DROP = 2` blood, where the devil stood. Whoever walks over it first takes all of it.
- `Hotel.killed(world, devil)` (about line 931) ends the fight and calls `Squad.leave`.
- `Squad.leave` (about line 153) pays `Squad.reward(player, saved)` to every id in `h.run.inside` who is online and
  alive: `REWARD_XP = 150` and `REWARD_GOLD = 4` per rescued hunter. `run.inside` is everyone in the hotel, fighting
  or not. A dead or down fighter gets nothing.
- `Hotel.java` about line 890 builds the `fighters` list for `Shaft.tick` (alive, above local y 28.5, in
  `run.inside`). That is "standing in the shaft", not "fought".
- The contract's holder is `HomeBlockEntity.holder`, one per hotel. `Hotel.java` lines 727 and 770 check
  `HotelRules.ONE_CONTRACT_PER_DEVIL` against it; `holderStillHolds` (line 789) frees it when the holder is online
  without the contract, and treats an offline holder as still holding. So two hotels can give two contracts.
- From memory, test first: a dead player's inventory is not carried to the respawned player (unless
  `keepInventory`), so paying a dead player straight into their inventory loses it.

## Build

1. **Who fought.** Keep a set of player ids on the hotel's `Run`, added to wherever a player's damage lands on the
   devil, the core, a shaft head or a shaft arm. Clear it when a fight starts and when the hotel resets. A tamed
   animal's or a summon's hit counts for its owner only if that's one line; otherwise leave it.
2. **Owed pay.** A small saved store for the whole server (a `PersistentState` on the overworld): player id → items
   and experience owed. Pay it the first tick that player is online, alive and not down (`Fallen`, `Downed`): items
   by `offerOrDrop`, with one line on the action bar. It survives a restart and a log-out.
3. **Loot per fighter.** When the devil has collapsed, each fighter is owed the whole drop (3 flesh, 2 blood)
   through the store, so a living fighter gets it at once. Nothing is dropped on the floor when anyone fought. If
   nobody is on the list (a `/kill`, a test), keep today's glowing pile.
4. **Pay for who fought.** `Squad.leave` pays the rescue reward through the same store to everyone on the fought
   list, alive or not, online or not, and to nobody else. Keep `Squad.reward`'s numbers and its message.
5. **One contract per world.** Move the holder into a server-wide saved store keyed by the contract's id. Both
   checks and `holderStillHolds` read it. A hotel saved with an old `holder` copies it into the store when it loads,
   if the store has none. With the rule off, behave as today.
6. **A flaky test.** `nothingIsOfferedInTheLoop` in
   `src/gametest/java/io/github/kortev/shootingstar/test/HotelGameTests.java` checks at tick 1340 with
   `tickLimit = 1500` and timed out once when the machine was busy. Raise the limit to 1800; change nothing else in it.

Don't touch the client renderers, the film (`HotelSelfTest.java`), or `ShowcaseSelfTest.java`.

## The four checks

- Show: nothing seen changes, except that the loot no longer lies on the floor.
- Minecraft: death and respawn, `keepInventory` on and off, a full inventory (`offerOrDrop`), a restart with pay
  still owed. Test each.
- Fun: alone, the only change is loot in the pack with a line saying so. Every existing test keeps its meaning;
  ones that looked for the pile on the floor now look in the pack, and one still covers the pile with no fighters.
- Other people: an idle second player inside gets nothing; two fighters each get the whole drop; a fighter who
  died gets theirs after respawning; a fighter who logged out gets theirs on joining.
