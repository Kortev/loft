# 0.1c The Eternity Devil: the big pass (parked, not started)

Parked 2026-11-10. The arena (0.1b) is merged and works. kortev and Fable agree it isn't very fun yet and that this
devil is a hard one to make. kortev: "we can do the other stuff too and do a big pass." So other packages go first.

## What kortev said (2026-11-10)

- On the arena film: "It's like the animations for like the arms. At least for Chainsaw Man, like that. I don't
  know what it would look like if you're not Chainsaw Man, but like that too."
- Fable's reading (not confirmed): the fight is nonstop slashing, and the slashing itself looks stiff. As Chainsaw
  Man your saw arms should really swing (wide, alternating, both arms and the head saw), in first person and for
  whoever watches you. With a sword there should be a matching flurry. The devil's own arms (slam, grab) are the
  second candidate: they move like poles. The big pass does both.

## Fable's picks for the pass (from the arena's weak points)

1. **The slashing.** Arm animations as above. This helps every fight in the mod, not only this one, so it can be
   pulled forward as its own package.
2. **Shorter.** About 90 seconds with saws, not 4 minutes.
3. **Frenzy.** Pain only sticks while you keep cutting and drains when you stop: nonstop slashing as a rule.
4. **Punishes that last.** A dodged arm or head that you cut is torn off for good; the room visibly loses parts.
5. **The room.** The walls are a red block checkerboard; they should read as flesh. The squeeze is lumpy. Close
   cuts fill the view. The fall through the mouth is only 1.5 s.
6. Hits from two overlapping attacks with no readable tell: remove.
