## The new direction (Fable's pick, 2026-11-11): a different kind of fight, not better animations

Why it's boring: it copies the show's staging (cut it nonstop until it gives up). That is an endurance fight:
one target that doesn't move, hold attack for four minutes. Arm swings (0.1d) make Chainsaw Man look better
everywhere, but they don't fix this, and they do nothing for a player with a sword.

Take the show's rule instead of its staging: **the devil fears pain, and when it hurts enough it shows its
heart** [check on the wiki before the brief]. So:

- **Damage only counts on the heart.** The heart is hidden until the devil is hurt.
- **You hurt it by punishing its attacks.** Each arm, face or bite has a tell; dodge it and the limb is stuck
  for a moment; cut it then and it is torn off for good (two or three hits with anything). The room visibly
  loses parts. This is the same for a sword and for saws, so the fight is fair without being Chainsaw Man.
- **After enough limbs it screams and the heart comes out** for a short window. Three rounds, each faster,
  the room squeezing smaller. About 90 s with saws, about 2.5 min with an iron sword.
- Chainsaw Man's edge is speed and reach in the heart window, not a different fight.
- Starts when a builder slot frees (four are running today). Full brief written then, with the picks below.

## Fable's earlier picks for the pass (from the arena's weak points)

1. **The slashing.** Arm animations as above. This helps every fight in the mod, not only this one, so it can be
   pulled forward as its own package.
2. **Shorter.** About 90 seconds with saws, not 4 minutes.
3. **Frenzy.** Pain only sticks while you keep cutting and drains when you stop: nonstop slashing as a rule.
4. **Punishes that last.** A dodged arm or head that you cut is torn off for good; the room visibly loses parts.
5. **The room.** The walls are a red block checkerboard; they should read as flesh. The squeeze is lumpy. Close
   cuts fill the view. The fall through the mouth is only 1.5 s.
6. Hits from two overlapping attacks with no readable tell: remove.
