## The video (about 35 s, 1080x1920, no HUD, no player hand)

Horror rules: you see parts before the whole; quiet, then sudden; it never does anything cute; no fight.

1. **Night, the edge of a village.** Fog, one lantern. Pochita, set down, stops and growls at the dark. (4 s)
2. **Low in the grass.** Far off between the trees, a cluster of pale glints. They blink, out of sync. (4 s)
3. **A hand.** Close: human fingers press into the dirt one by one. Another hand lands behind it. Only the
   patter. (4 s)
4. **The shape.** From behind a tree, a slow push-in: its outline against the moon, eight arms working. (5 s)
5. **A window.** From inside a house, past a sleeping villager: eyes rise into the window, wandering. (5 s)
6. **One eye.** Very close. It rolls, twitches, then snaps to the camera and the pupil shrinks. Cut wider:
   every eye is on you. Silence. (5 s)
7. **The mouth.** It parts from top to bottom, teeth, a wet breath, strands of juice. (4 s)
8. **Black.** One beat. Then it's coming at the camera, hands slapping the ground; cut to black on the lunge.
   White text: "The Tomato Devil". (4 s)

- Sound, synthesised only: a low drone, the hand patter, wet breathing, a heartbeat that stops at shot 6, one
  hard hit on the lunge. No music from the show.
- Light: night, but every shot has a light in it (moon, lantern, window, eyeshine) so the thing is always
  readable. Dark is not the same as unlit.
- **Grading and post effects** (kortev, 2026-11-10: "maybe some post effects too with color grading and
  stuff"). Done on the finished frames with ffmpeg, so no game code. Fable's picks: a cold, drained grade (greens
  and blues pulled down, blacks a little lifted and teal) with the red of the devil and its juice left strong, so
  it is the only colour in the frame; film grain; a dark vignette; a slight colour fringe at the edges; a soft
  glow on the eyeshine and the lantern; a small focus breath on the close-ups; a few frames of flicker on the
  lunge. Keep it light enough that the thing stays readable. Deliver `grade_before_after.jpg` (three shots,
  ungraded beside graded). Only the horror short is graded: the played fight film stays as the game looks.
- Camera: scripted paths in a selftest of its own (`TomatoSelfTest`, a `cinematic` target), slow moves, a
  narrow FOV for close-ups. The devil is posed with its real animations, no special model.

## What was built

   shading smoothed over the solid cells, `soft` and `blend`); veins, fibres and the wet sheen stay. Pochita sitting is let down 0.1

2. **Take: done** (`-Ptarget=cinematic`, `test/TomatoCinematic.java`): a cottage (a window east, a lantern on a barrel under it, a
   villager asleep), a lantern post, a dark-oak wood, a lone tree, grass; night at 14600 (full moon, 30 degrees up in the east); a
   test-side fog per shot (`CinematicFogMixin`) and lens (`ReelLensMixin`). Each shot has a lead that is cut; `cinematic_marks.json`
   gives every shot's frames and its moment (rise, snap, mouth, slap). Staged: the devil's speed, its mind off where posed, its noticing
   set by hand, Pochita moved by hand.
3. **Shots** (the cut, 37.5 s): 1 Pochita trots to the lantern post and stops facing the dark (3.8 s); 2 the devil far between the trees,
   graded down to its eyes (4.0); 3 its hands coming down by the lens, the last one huge (4.4); 4 from behind the lone tree its outline
   crosses the moon (4.6); 5 changed: it does not walk up but rises into the window past the sleeper (its real coming-back animation

4. **Post: done** (`tools/tomato_creepy.py`): grade, glow, vignette, fringe, grain, focus breath, flicker and flash on the frames; a
   synthesised score (drone, crickets that stop, Pochita's growl, a heartbeat that stops at the snap, the hands placed on the game's own

   frame of every shot, ungraded and graded, the stems and the capture are kept in `work/tomato/creepy_frames/` for the teaser (to be
   cut after kortev has seen the short: 6-8 s, from these frames, no new filming); shots 2 and 4 were filmed long for it (10 and 12 s).
