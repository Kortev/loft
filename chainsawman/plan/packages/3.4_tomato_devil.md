# 3.4 The Tomato Devil (the weak first devil), and the `devil/` lane (0.6)

Branch `tomato-devil`, cut from `claude/chainsaw-man`.

## What the player gets

A devil with a body: a big red tomato covered in eyes that walks on eight human arms. It is weak, a first-night
fight for one player in stone or iron gear, about 30-45 seconds. Kill it and its seeds scatter: burn them or it
comes back.

## Decided

- kortev (2026-11-09): design for someone who installs and just plays; a common devil needs one move with a look
  worth filming. kortev (2026-11-10), on the arena: the arm animations are what's missing.
- kortev's rule: Fable decides, builds, and kortev judges the finished thing. Everything below is Fable's pick.
- The show never shows it fight (fact sheet), so its moves are ours. Its looks and its seed rule are the show's.

## Facts

`chainsawman/bible/tomato_devil.md` (checked, to chapter 52): round red body, many bulging eyes in every direction,
a vertical mouth down the middle, stands on eight human-like arms, big enough to stand on. Weak. It never speaks.
It comes back to life from its seeds, so the seeds must be burned. Its body sells (the yen and the shop come later).

## Build

1. **The lane (0.6).** New package `chainsawman/.../devil/` with its own registration class, called by one line
   from the mod's init; client side under `client/devil/`. The Tomato's items, entities and sounds register there.
2. **The body.** About 2.6 blocks across, riding 1.5 blocks up on its arms. Eyes that follow the nearest player
   and blink. A vertical mouth that opens. Cuts leave gashes that stay; juice sprays; at low health eyes burst.
3. **The arms are the look.** Eight jointed arms (shoulder, elbow, hand) in a walking gait, alternating fours:
   hands plant flat and don't slide while planted, elbows bend with the load. They rear, tuck, wave and curl as
   below. No stiff poles. Judge this on stills and a slow clip before anything else.
4. **Moves, each with a tell of 12 ticks or more:**
   - *Slap.* Rears on its back four arms, the front arms go up, then slap down (4 damage, 3 blocks in front).
   - *Roll.* Tucks its arms in and rocks back, then rolls straight 10 blocks (5 damage, a knock aside). Into a
     wall or a tree: it lies on its back 40 ticks, arms waving, and hits on it count 1.5.
   - *Seed spit.* The mouth opens wide, then five seeds in a fan (2 damage each, 8 blocks).
   Health 60. It keeps coming at walking speed; a player can outrun it.
5. **Death and the seeds.** It bursts: a big splat of juice (particles and a short-lived stain, no block changes),
   a carcass item, and five seeds that fly out up to 6 blocks and stick in the ground, glowing faintly. A seed
   burns when fire, lava or a lit campfire touches it, or when a player uses a torch (used up), flint and steel
   or a fire charge on it. If any seed is left after 60 s, one swells for 10 s (the tell) and the devil climbs
   out of it; the other seeds wither. Each comeback scatters one seed fewer, so a line dies out by itself. When
   the seeds land, one action-bar line: "Its seeds are in the ground. Burn them."
6. **In the world.** Rare, at night, on the surface of the overworld, never within 128 blocks of another one.
   Seeds don't despawn. If the player's Pochita is within 32 blocks of one, he barks and faces it (only if that is
   a small change; else leave it and say so). The Chainsaw Man form's saws must cut it like any mob.
7. **Sound**, synthesised: wet hand-steps, a gurgle, the roll, the burst, a seed's hiss as it burns.
8. **The film.** A played fight, first person, HUD on, normal FOV, at dusk so every frame is lit: meet it, dodge
   the slap, get hit by the roll once, bait a roll into a tree and punish it, dodge the seeds, kill it, burn four
   seeds with a torch, miss one on purpose, it comes back, kill it again, burn the lot. About 60 s.

Don't touch the hotel code, `Squad.java`, or `ShowcaseSelfTest.java`.

## The four checks

- Show: looks and the seed rule are the fact sheet's; nothing from the story is staged. Nothing past chapter 52.
- Minecraft: a first-night player has torches, so a torch can burn a seed. No block is changed by the fight.
  Seeds and the devil survive a save and a reload.
- Fun: every hit has a tell; the roll into a wall is the player's reward; a missed seed is a second fight, not a
  punishment that snowballs (one comes back, and fewer seeds each time).
- Other people: seeds left near a village can't multiply (one comeback per death); anyone can burn anyone's seed.

## What was built

1. **Lane: done.** `devil/Devils` registers the Tomato Devil, its seed and its spat seed, the carcass item, a spawn egg and the
   sounds; one line in `ChainsawMan.onInitialize`. Client: `client/devil/DevilsClient`, one line in `ChainsawManClient`.
2. **Body: done.** Baked by `tools/tomato_devil_3d.py` with the Eternity Devil's voxel kit and drawn with `FleshMesh` (opened up to
   load other meshes). 2.6 across, its middle 2.45 up (underside about 1.4 up), hit box 2.4 x 3.5. 26 bulging eyes that follow the
   nearest player and blink on their own; a vertical mouth whose two lips open (teeth inside); a green calyx. Cuts stay as gashes
   where the player stood; juice sprays on every hit; under 40% health a third of the eyes are burst, under 22% two thirds.
3. **Arms: done.** Eight arms, each upper arm, forearm, hand and fingers, solved as two bones (elbow out and up). Alternating fours:
   a group lifts when its hands have fallen behind (more the faster it walks), lands ahead by as much; planted hands stay put in
   the world; the body sags onto the four standing. Hands hang and curl as they lift and land flat. Posed: reared with the front
   four high, slapping down; tucked round the body to roll; waving in the air on its back; curled in when dying; flung out of
   the ground when it comes back. `work/tomato/arms.jpg` is one walk cycle from the side.
4. **Moves: done, two numbers changed.** Slap: tell 16 ticks, hits 3 later, 4 damage, reaches **4.2** from its middle (3 blocks
   past its body: further than a sword reaches it, else a player could hit it from outside the slap forever). Roll: tell 18
   (aimed only in its first 10), 0.6 a tick for 10 blocks, 5 damage and knocked aside; into a wall or tree: 40 ticks on its back,
   hits count 1.5, then 14 ticks getting up. It only starts a roll from 5.5 to 8.5 blocks (so a roll at you reaches you).
   Spit: tell 14 (aimed in its first 6), five seeds 13 degrees apart, 2 damage, 8 blocks; slapped at twice running it spits
   instead. Health 60, walk about 3.2 blocks/s. Its first move waits 2 s after it appears.
5. **Death and seeds: done, one number changed.** It curls up and swells for **30 ticks** (not the game's 16: the camera and the
   player get to step back), bursts (juice, pulp, a juice stain on the ground for 15 s, no blocks changed), drops its carcass,
   and five seeds fly 3 to 6 blocks and stick, glowing. Fire, lava, a lit campfire, anything that sets it alight, a torch (used up), flint
   and steel or a fire charge burns a seed. After 60 s one swells 10 s and the devil climbs out; the others wither. Each
   comeback has one seed fewer (5, 4, 3, 2, 1, 0). Burning the swelling seed stops the comeback. The action-bar line shows when
   the first seed lands.
6. **World: done.** Monsters' spawn list in every overworld biome at weight 2, only at night by the clock, nothing above it,
   on solid ground, never within 128 blocks of another. Seeds never despawn; a devil that came back is kept. Pochita barks and
   faces a devil or seed within 32 blocks (every 10 s while it is there): a few lines in `PochitaEntity.tick`. The saws cut it;
   `Saw.tearApart` now lets it burst instead of discarding it (one `if` at its top).
7. **Sound: done,** in `tools/gen_chainsaw_sounds.py`: step, gurgle, slap, tuck, roll, thud, spit, hurt, burst, hiss, swell, and
   Pochita's bark.
8. **Film: done** (`-Pselftest=tomato -Ptarget=played`), 70 s, at dusk. Staged: the left seed's minute brought on and its swell
   cut to its last 4 s; the comeback's health set to 24; your health kept from running out. Measured kills from first sight
   (a scripted player, `-Ptarget=played-stone|iron|saws`): stone sword 15.3 s, iron 14.2 s, the saws 6.0 s; in the film, with
   the roll taken and baited, 45 s.

Heart XP (asked for during the build): the Tomato is in the `devils` tag and in a new `weak_devils` tag; `HeartLevel.xpFor` gives
a weak devil `WEAK_KILL_XP` = 8 instead of 40 (a comeback the same), so one Tomato leaves the heart at level 1. Nothing else in
`HeartLevel` changed; the retune is a later package.
