# 0.1b The bottom of the shaft is a boss arena (attack and dodge)

Branch `devil-arena`, cut from `claude/chainsaw-man` (round 3 and 0.7b are merged there).

## What the player gets

The floor turns, you fall down the corridor and into the devil's mouth, and you land inside it: a small arena of
flesh. It attacks with things you can see coming and dodge; you cut it the whole time; when it misses
you get a free cut. It can't be killed, only hurt until it gives up.

## Decided

- kortev, 2026-11-09: "falling in should be like a little boss arena... You have to attack and then dodge stuff."
  Nonstop slashing; it isn't killed, the pain makes it give up. No day counter, no anime staging.
- kortev, same day: "the mouth is on the floor, and then when you fall in, that's what brings you to the arena."
  So the mouth is the way in and the arena is inside the devil. Nobody waits on a ledge (kortev disliked the
  drag-in), and nobody is "swallowed" in the arena: they already are.
- kortev, same day: its heart isn't on this floor, which is why it can't be killed; you make it reveal itself.
  And: "You decide your questions, and I'll just tell you how it is when it's done."
- kortev on the round 3 film, same day: "this just looks bad and boring." Fable's reading of its stills: half the
  frames are near black or one texture filling the screen; you can't tell what is attacking or whether a cut
  landed; the swallowed screen is red curtains; the fight is one 16 s loop. The look rules below answer that.
- So everything below is Fable's pick and isn't sent for approval; kortev judges the finished fight. It reverses the old rule in `Shaft.java`'s header ("small, quick, hard to
  avoid; never a big telegraphed slam"): now **no damage without a tell**.

## Build

1. **The way in and the arena.** The shaft's bottom is the mouth (a pit with lips and teeth in a floor of fused
   arms), wide enough that everyone who falls goes in; an arm takes in anyone who catches a ledge. Past the mouth:
   a flesh floor about 17 x 12 (as big as fits the footprint; under the mouth if there's room, else moved during
   the dark of the fall), at least 6 high, flesh walls, the mouth's light overhead, no pit in the floor. Players
   land unhurt. The old pit-swallow state (`CUT_NEED`, `PAIN_BURST`) goes.
2. **Attacks, each with a tell of at least 12 ticks that a player can read and step out of:**
   - *Arm slam.* A lane 3 wide swells with a lit seam, from a wall toward a player; then an arm slams along it
     (6 damage, a shove toward the mouth). The arm lies there 30 ticks.
   - *Head lunge.* A head rises 4-6 blocks from a player, rears back with its eyes on them, then lunges straight
     6 blocks (5 damage). If it misses it hangs out 25 ticks.
   - *The squeeze.* Half the room's walls bulge and their seams light, then that half clenches shut (6 damage, a
     shove to the middle). Stand in the other half. The bulged wall stays soft 40 ticks: cuts there count 1.5.
   - *The grab.* A hand rises open at a wall, then sweeps low across half the floor. Caught: it lifts and crushes
     you (2 damage a second) until you or a friend cut the arm.
   - *Creeping flesh* stays as it is: slow, pushes you to keep moving.
3. **Cutting.** The walls and the floor's growths can be cut at any time for pain, so there's always
   something to hit. A head or arm that has just missed is worth 1.5 times as much (replace `MASS_BONUS` with
   this). Heads and arms that are cut down grow back.
4. **Escalation.** One attack at a time at first. Past a third of the pain bar two overlap; past two thirds the gaps
   shorten and its cries get worse. More players: more attacks at once, each aimed at a different player.
   On the first cut that lands it says once (new lang key `message.chainsawman.devil.noheart`): "My heart is not
   here. You cannot kill me." So a player who hasn't seen the show knows the bar is pain, not health.
5. **Length.** Tune pain so a player with the chainsaws takes about 3-5 minutes and an iron sword with a friend
   about twice that. Use the round 3 numbers as the start.
6. **The film.** A played take with the HUD on (reuse round 3's fighting-player helper): fall into the mouth, dodge each
   attack once, get caught by one, punish the misses, get grabbed and cut free, the bar fills, it breaks.

## The look (comes first; build and judge it on stills before tuning any damage)

- **Never dark.** The arena is lit from inside the flesh: glowing veins and seams in the walls and floor, the
  mouth's light overhead. Block light 10 or more on every floor block, and the fall down the corridor is lit too.
  No near-black frame anywhere in the film, the ending included.
- **Readable at a distance.** Every attack starts 4 or more blocks from the player and is seen whole: pale grey
  heads and arms against dark red flesh, tells in bright orange. Never a screen filled by one texture.
- **Every cut shows.** A cut leaves a lit wound that stays a few seconds, blood sprays toward the player, the
  flesh flinches, the devil cries out, the pain bar visibly jumps. A miss by the devil looks like a miss: the arm
  or head lies stunned and exposed, glowing where it should be cut.
- **It gets wilder.** At a third and two thirds of the bar the room itself changes (more veins lit, walls
  heaving faster, more heads watching), so minute three doesn't look like minute one.
- **The ending is a sight.** Full pain: the whole room convulses, the light flares, the core is pushed up in the
  middle in plain view. Not a contract screen over darkness.

Don't touch `Squad.java`, the loot or the contract code, or `ShowcaseSelfTest.java`.

## The four checks

- Show: heads, arms, a mouth, a stomach, pain. Nothing staged from the story.
- Minecraft: the bigger chamber fits the reserved space and every block goes back; no fall damage on landing;
  pull and carry work by velocity and don't fight the anti-cheat "moved too quickly" check. Test each.
- Fun: every hit taken was avoidable; a miss by the devil is a reward; no stretch over 3 s with nothing to cut.
- Other people: attacks spread across players; a grabbed friend can be helped by cutting.

## What was built

   orange inside; under it the arena, 17 x 12 inside, 7 high (`Shaft.AX0..AX1`, `AZ0..AZ1`, `FLOOR` = SY + 6, `HIGH`),
   flesh walls two thick, no pit. The turn drops everyone 24 blocks over the mouth (`FALL_FROM`); the fall is held to
   0.9 blocks a tick (about 1.5 s, soft landing). Anyone on a ledge 16 ticks is taken in by a rising arm; anyone on the
   fused arms round the hole is pushed in. `CUT_NEED`, `PAIN_BURST`, `swallowTicks`, the swallow state, `HotelHud.swallowed`

2. Done. Slam (lane 3 wide lit 22 ticks, 6 damage, shove to the middle, lies 30), head (rises 5 blocks off, its line fixed
   and lit from tick 9, lunges 6.5 at tick 24, 5 damage, hangs 25 if it missed), squeeze (half's walls bulge with lit veins
   34 ticks, then clench: 6 damage to x < 17.5 or > 21.5, shove to the middle, walls soft 40 ticks at 1.5x), grab (hand rises
