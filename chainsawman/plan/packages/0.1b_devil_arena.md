## What the player gets

The floor turns, you fall down the corridor, and you land in the devil's stomach: a small arena of flesh with its
mouth in the middle. It attacks with things you can see coming and dodge; you cut it the whole time; when it misses
you get a free cut. It can't be killed, only hurt until it gives up.

## Build

1. **The arena.** A flesh floor about 17 x 12 (as big as fits the hotel's footprint), at least 6 high, flesh walls,
   the mouth (the pit, with its lips) in the middle so you circle it. Players fall down the shaft and land on the
   flesh unhurt. The ledges stay as scenery; an arm still takes anyone who stays on one.
2. **Attacks, each with a tell of at least 12 ticks that a player can read and step out of:**
   - *Arm slam.* A lane 3 wide swells with a lit seam, from a wall toward a player; then an arm slams along it
     (6 damage, a shove toward the mouth). The arm lies there 30 ticks.
   - *Head lunge.* A head rises 4-6 blocks from a player, rears back with its eyes on them, then lunges straight
     6 blocks (5 damage). If it misses it hangs out 25 ticks.
   - *The inhale.* The lips lift and blood streams toward the mouth, then it pulls everyone in for 50 ticks.
     Sprinting away holds your ground. Pulled in means swallowed (as today: cut your way out for a burst of pain).
   - *The grab.* A hand rises open at a wall, then sweeps low across half the floor. Caught: it carries you to the
     mouth over 40 ticks; hitting the arm makes it drop you.
   - *Creeping flesh* stays as it is: slow, pushes you to keep moving.
3. **Cutting.** The walls, the lips and the floor's growths can be cut at any time for pain, so there's always
   something to hit. A head or arm that has just missed is worth 1.5 times as much (replace `MASS_BONUS` with
   this). Heads and arms that are cut down grow back.
4. **Escalation.** One attack at a time at first. Past a third of the pain bar two overlap; past two thirds the gaps
   shorten and its cries get worse. More players: more attacks at once, each aimed at a different player.
5. **Length.** Tune pain so a player with the chainsaws takes about 3-5 minutes and an iron sword with a friend
   about twice that. Use the round 3 numbers as the start.
6. **The film.** A played take with the HUD on (reuse round 3's fighting-player helper): fall in, dodge each attack
   once, get caught by one, punish the misses, get swallowed and cut out, the bar fills, it breaks.

The look comes first: build each tell as a big readable visual before tuning its damage.

## The four checks

- Show: heads, arms, a mouth, a stomach, pain. Nothing staged from the story.
- Minecraft: the bigger chamber fits the reserved space and every block goes back; no fall damage on landing;

- Fun: every hit taken was avoidable; a miss by the devil is a reward; no stretch over 3 s with nothing to cut.
- Other people: attacks spread across players; a swallowed or carried friend can be helped by cutting.
