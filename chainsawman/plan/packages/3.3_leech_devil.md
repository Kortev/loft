## The look (from the fact sheet; build it like the Tomato was built)

The Tomato Devil's model and texture come from `tools/tomato_devil_3d.py` and its renderer under
`client/devil/`. Follow that pipeline with a new script and new classes.

- A big wrinkled, sagging body on **four legs**, about 3 blocks tall at the back and 5 long, plus **two long
  tentacle arms** at the front. **Every limb ends in a round sucker mouth** (a ring of small teeth, pale lip,
  dark red inside) that opens and closes.
- **The face is one large mouth full of blocky teeth.** No eyes. **Long black hair** hangs over it and drags.
- Three pairs of udder-like glands under the body. Colour as in the anime: dark, wet, real-leech colours (olive
  black, a paler belly, a wet sheen), not the manga's yellow.
- Creepy by itself: the suckers pulse out of time with each other; the body ripples when it breathes; the long
  tongue slides out to taste the air; the hair sways; it turns its eyeless face to you by sound (a slight lag).

## The fight (Fable's design; numbers are first guesses in named constants)

Health 160. Slower than a player sprinting, faster than one walking. Never two attacks at once. Always a gap
after an attack in which it can be hit. Aim: an iron sword wins in 60-90 s if you dodge; a stone sword can.

1. **The tongue.** Tell (16 ticks): it rears, the mouth gapes, the tongue coils back, a wet rising sound. The
   aim locks at the end of the tell. Then the tongue lances out in a straight line up to 10 blocks, in 3 ticks,
   and stays out 8 ticks (cut it then for extra damage). 8 damage and a short stagger. Sidestep to dodge.
2. **The latch (its own trick).** Tell (14 ticks): one tentacle arm coils back and its sucker opens with a
   smack. It whips out up to 7 blocks. On a hit it **sticks to the player**: they are slowed, lose 1 health
   every 10 ticks, and each pulse **heals the devil 4** (blood visibly pulses along the arm, a sucking sound,
   red at the screen's edge for that player). **Cut the arm to get free:** 3 hits on the arm (or 12 damage)
   tears it loose; the sucker drops off, the arm is stunned 6 s, the devil shrieks. A friend can cut it too.
   It lets go by itself after 6 s. This is the only way it heals: the show's devils heal on blood.
3. **The trample.** Only when the player is 8+ blocks away. Tell (20 ticks): it lowers its body and paws the
   ground with a front sucker. It runs a straight line 14 blocks; 6 damage and a throw. It skids and is open for
   30 ticks after. Step aside.
4. **Death.** It sags and folds; the suckers keep opening and closing for a few seconds, slower, then stop.
   Drops a new item, `leech_sucker` (a devil's part; just the item and its texture for now).

Voice: a soft sing-song hum (synthesised, no words) while it circles; it cuts off dead at each tell, and turns
to a shriek when hurt. Footsteps are wet slaps.

## What was built

Built on `leech-devil` (Fable 5.2 brief, built 2026-11-11).

- **Model** (`tools/leech_devil_3d.py` -> `leech_body_*`, `leech_parts_*`): a wrinkled, ringed olive-black body (paler belly,
  wet sheen, dull spots down the back) 3 high at the hump and about 5.5 long; three pairs of udder glands under it; a head
  that is one wide lipped slit of interlocking blocky teeth (a hinged jaw opens it onto a dark red throat and tongue), no
  eyes, six locks of long black hair over it (the side locks drag on the ground). Parts: thigh, shin, tentacle segment,
  sucker (pale lip, a ring of 12 teeth, dark red inside), tongue segment and tip.
- **Renderer** (`client/devil/LeechDevilRenderer`, `LeechBody`, `LeechClient`): four legs by a two-bone solve on a trot
  gait (diagonal pairs) with wet-slap steps; two tentacle arms as 12-segment curves that thin as they stretch; every sucker
  opens and shuts out of time with the others; the body breathes; the face turns to you late (re-heard every 7 ticks); the
  hair sways and trails; the tongue tastes the air now and then. Red creeps in from the screen's edges, throbbing with each
  pulse, while its arm drinks from you. The torn-off sucker drops and gapes on the ground.
- **Fight** (`devil/LeechDevilEntity`, `LeechPartEntity`): health 160, hide 14 armour (added: see below), about 5 b/s; it
  circles you crab-wise with its face on you, humming, and pauses after every move. Tongue: 16-tick tell (rears, gapes),
  its aim follows where you were 6 ticks ago and locks at the end of the tell; lances up to 10 blocks in 3 ticks, held out
  8 (a cut on it counts 1.5x), 8 damage and a stagger. Latch: 14-tick tell (arm coils back, sucker smacks open), aim set at
  tick 7 from the arm's shoulder, whips out 7 blocks; stuck: slowed, 1 a pulse every 10 ticks, it heals 4; cut the arm
  (3 hits of 2.5+, or 12 damage; anyone can) and it tears loose, shrieks and reels back, the arm hangs stunned 6 s; it
  lets go by itself after 6 s. Trample: only from 8+ blocks, 20-tick tell (lowers, paws three times), runs 14 blocks, 6
  damage and a throw, skids open 30 ticks. Death: sags and folds over 4.5 s, suckers slowing to a stop; drops
  `leech_sucker`. `/summon chainsawman:leech_devil` and a spawn egg; no world spawning.
- **Sounds** (`tools/gen_leech_sounds.py`, 16 files): three sing-song hum phrases (cut dead at each tell by a stop-sound
  packet), tongue tell slurp, lance, smack, whip, suck, tear, shriek (its hurt sound), paw, trample, skid, steps, death, taste.
- **Tests** `LeechGameTests` (7): every move's tell; the tongue misses who stepped off its line and hits who stood still,
  and a cut on it counts more; the latch drains, heals and slows; three cuts free you and stun the arm; it lets go after 6 s;
  the trample only from 8+ (one devil at 5 blocks never, one at 12 does); it drops its sucker and goes.
- **Film** `LeechSelfTest` / `LeechFight` (`-Ptarget=played`, also `model`, `played-iron`, `played-stone`):
  `work/leech/fight.mp4`, `contact_sheet.jpg`, `model.jpg`, `poses.jpg`, takes and stills under `raw/`.

Changes from the brief, decided while building: the hide (armour 14) so an iron sword takes about a minute (with plain 160
health the scripted fighter killed it in 31-36 s); the tongue's late aim (with an aim that tracks you to the lock, a
3-tick lance can't be sidestepped); the latch aim locks 7 ticks before the whip; its hitbox is 4 wide (the head sticks far
out past a 3-wide box, so you ended up inside its face) and anyone inside the body's long shape is eased out.
