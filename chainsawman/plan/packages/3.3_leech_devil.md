## The look (from the fact sheet; build it like the Tomato was built)

The Tomato Devil's model and texture come from `tools/tomato_devil_3d.py` and its renderer under
`client/devil/`. Follow that pipeline with a new script and new classes.

- A big wrinkled, sagging body on **four legs**, about 3 blocks tall at the back and 5 long, plus **two long
  tentacle arms** at the front. **Every limb ends in a round sucker mouth** (a ring of small teeth, pale lip,
  dark red inside) that opens and closes.
- **The face is one large mouth full of blocky teeth.** No eyes. **Long black hair** hangs over it and drags.
- Three pairs of udder-like glands under the body. Colour as in the anime: dark, wet, real-leech colours (olive
  black, a paler belly, a wet sheen), not the manga's yellow.
- Creepy by itself: the suckers pulse out of time with each other; the body ripples when it breathes; the long
  tongue slides out to taste the air; the hair sways; it turns its eyeless face to you by sound (a slight lag).

## The fight (Fable's design; numbers are first guesses in named constants)

Health 160. Slower than a player sprinting, faster than one walking. Never two attacks at once. Always a gap
after an attack in which it can be hit. Aim: an iron sword wins in 60-90 s if you dodge; a stone sword can.

1. **The tongue.** Tell (16 ticks): it rears, the mouth gapes, the tongue coils back, a wet rising sound. The
   aim locks at the end of the tell. Then the tongue lances out in a straight line up to 10 blocks, in 3 ticks,
   and stays out 8 ticks (cut it then for extra damage). 8 damage and a short stagger. Sidestep to dodge.
2. **The latch (its own trick).** Tell (14 ticks): one tentacle arm coils back and its sucker opens with a
   smack. It whips out up to 7 blocks. On a hit it **sticks to the player**: they are slowed, lose 1 health
   every 10 ticks, and each pulse **heals the devil 4** (blood visibly pulses along the arm, a sucking sound,
   red at the screen's edge for that player). **Cut the arm to get free:** 3 hits on the arm (or 12 damage)
   tears it loose; the sucker drops off, the arm is stunned 6 s, the devil shrieks. A friend can cut it too.
   It lets go by itself after 6 s. This is the only way it heals: the show's devils heal on blood.
3. **The trample.** Only when the player is 8+ blocks away. Tell (20 ticks): it lowers its body and paws the
   ground with a front sucker. It runs a straight line 14 blocks; 6 damage and a throw. It skids and is open for
   30 ticks after. Step aside.
4. **Death.** It sags and folds; the suckers keep opening and closing for a few seconds, slower, then stop.
   Drops a new item, `leech_sucker` (a devil's part; just the item and its texture for now).

Voice: a soft sing-song hum (synthesised, no words) while it circles; it cuts off dead at each tell, and turns
to a shriek when hurt. Footsteps are wet slaps.
