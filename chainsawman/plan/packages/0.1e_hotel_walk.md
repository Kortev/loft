# 0.1e The hotel can be walked

Branch `hotel-walk`. Size M. Lane: `hotel/` (main and client).

The mod has a lair: a hotel whose eighth floor loops forever (the Eternity Devil's trap), with a boss fight deeper in. The owner, kortev, played it and said (2026-11-11): "The actual hotel, like the way it's supposed to be infinite of the teleportation is really bad. Going up the stairs, you can't go up the fucking stairs, you have to destroy the stairs." Also: "graphically, it's really bad. When you're in the devil, the blocks combined with the entity looks really bad. The arena is just like horrible."

This job fixes the first two and documents the rest. It does not redesign the fight or the arena.

1. The stairs. Reproduce first: a real survival player walking (holding forward, no jumping needed where stairs are stairs, no block broken) from the hotel's front door up every staircase a player is meant to use, and back down. Find why it can't be done (headroom, a blocking block, a step higher than a stair, a teleport or barrier pushing the player back, a door, anything) and fix it in the hotel's generation so every route a player is meant to take can be walked and sprinted, up and down, with two free blocks of headroom the whole way. Fix it for every hotel variant and rotation the mod can place, and for hotels already placed in old worlds if the mod has a way to repair or rebuild them (say what happens to old ones).
2. The loop. Walking the looping floor must feel like one endless building: when the player is moved, nothing may show it. No camera snap, no stutter or rubber-banding, no change of look direction, speed or sprint, no flicker of light or blocks, no sound cut, no fall or bump. The two places must look identical around the player at the moment of the move, the move must keep the player's exact offset, look and velocity (relative teleport flags, and client-side handling if the server move alone shows a hitch), and it must work while sprinting, jumping, going up and going down, and for two players each looping by themselves. Mobs, dropped items and a tamed Pochita following the player: say what happens to them and make it not look broken.
3. Document for the next job (no fixing): walk from the door to the start of the boss fight and through the fight's arena, and list every place that looks bad, especially where blocks meet the devil's entity/model (gaps, z-fighting, flat colour, different pixel sizes, lighting that doesn't match, the checkerboard walls). One still per problem.

Tests: a game test that walks the stair routes geometrically (every step on each route has a walkable rise and two free blocks above) for every variant/rotation; a game test that the loop move keeps offset, yaw, pitch and velocity within a tiny tolerance and works for two players; and a filmed self-test that really holds the forward key up and down the stairs and round the loop three times and fails if the player ends up in the wrong place or broke a block. All game tests must pass after your last edit: `./gradlew runGametest` from the repo root; count in `build/gametest/junit.xml` (it is one line: `grep -o '<testcase' | wc -l` and `grep -o '<failure' | wc -l`). 283 on your base. Known flaky, rerun once before investigating: `each_fighter_gets_the_whole_drop`, two form tests, a Beam missile test.

The film (real play: first person, HUD on, normal FOV, no grade, no cuts): the player walks in the front door, up the stairs, round the loop three times without stopping, and back down. 1080x1920. Follow how the existing hotel self-test films (`./gradlew runSelftest -Pselftest=hotel -Ptarget=<yours> -Pusername=Kortev -Pcapture=true -Pwidth=1080 -Pheight=1920 "-Pjvmargs=-Xmx2600m -XX:TieredStopAtLevel=1"`). ## What was built

1. Stairs. Cause (reproduced: the new walking test fails on the old plan, stopped on the first step with smooth quartz
   over the head): one flight of four steps a storey in a one-wide lane, the slab above opened over only the 2nd and 3rd
   step. Stepping from the 1st onto the 2nd, the head (1.8 tall, 0.6 wide, still over the 1st step's column) hit the slab
   over the 1st step: no way up without breaking it. Fix (`HotelPlan.stairs`): every storey the same U, two half flights:
   two steps up east by the corridor wall (z 3), a half landing across x 16-17, two steps back west by the front wall
   (z 1) to the next floor's landing strip (x 13, the door's side). The slab is open over everything but the strip and the
   top step; an iron-bar rail (z 2, x 14-15) runs bottom to top between the lanes (keeps you in a lane, closes what would
   be a hole down the whole well). Two free blocks over every step, and over the next step while your head is still over
   the last. One plan, four turns: all walked. Old worlds: `HomeBlockEntity.stairs` (saved; 0 if missing) - an older hotel
   gets the new well (x 13-17, z 1-3, ground to roof) rebuilt at its first tick with nobody alive in the well
   (`HotelBuilder.rebuildStairs`), lights put back if it is night. Squad exit moved to the landing strip.
2. Loop. The server move was already relative (every axis and the look marked relative: the client keeps its own place,
   look, speed, sprint and last-frame place; checked in the bytecode of 1.21.1's client). What showed it was what did not
   repeat: the stair door left open (13 vs 25), the squad and eyes being in one place only (popping in and out of the fog),
   the flesh re-made with new shapes at every lap (lumps changed at the move), lump shapes seeded per lump not per period,
   eye pupils and the server's look-to-shut using the real eye. Now: loop-floor doors shut themselves with nobody at them,
   and doors round where you come out copy those round where you were; flesh only added (old lumps stay, new grow in),
   shapes by place mod 12; a cut wall bleeds at its copies too and the hunt counts a copy's cut; `ClientLoop` (client,
   from the fog payload, which now carries origin and turn) draws every non-periodic thing in the corridor again at +-12
   and +-24 inside it, eyes looking at you from where they are drawn; a thing the server moves a period is moved on the
   client first (`HotelLoopMoveMixin` on the entity-position packet) so it does not slide 12 blocks.
   Followers: mobs, pets (Pochita), a fed survivor, xp orbs nearer where you were than where you come out (and nearer you
   than any other looping player) come with you, kept their speed, paths reset; they are put a period back if they near
   the corridor's real ends. Dropped items stay put but are kept in the window (moved a period when outside it), so
   walking always reaches the real one. Sitting survivors, flesh, eyes and the devil are not moved.
   Not changed: going down the stairs from the loop floor still puts you back at the corridor's start (the trap; a
   visible jump with the look reset). The film sets the devil away to walk down (said in its log).
