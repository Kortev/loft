# 0.1e The hotel can be walked

Branch `hotel-walk`. Size M. Lane: `hotel/` (main and client).

The mod has a lair: a hotel whose eighth floor loops forever (the Eternity Devil's trap), with a boss fight deeper in. The owner, kortev, played it and said (2026-11-11): "The actual hotel, like the way it's supposed to be infinite of the teleportation is really bad. Going up the stairs, you can't go up the fucking stairs, you have to destroy the stairs." Also: "graphically, it's really bad. When you're in the devil, the blocks combined with the entity looks really bad. The arena is just like horrible."

This job fixes the first two and documents the rest. It does not redesign the fight or the arena.

1. The stairs. Reproduce first: a real survival player walking (holding forward, no jumping needed where stairs are stairs, no block broken) from the hotel's front door up every staircase a player is meant to use, and back down. Find why it can't be done (headroom, a blocking block, a step higher than a stair, a teleport or barrier pushing the player back, a door, anything) and fix it in the hotel's generation so every route a player is meant to take can be walked and sprinted, up and down, with two free blocks of headroom the whole way. Fix it for every hotel variant and rotation the mod can place, and for hotels already placed in old worlds if the mod has a way to repair or rebuild them (say what happens to old ones).
2. The loop. Walking the looping floor must feel like one endless building: when the player is moved, nothing may show it. No camera snap, no stutter or rubber-banding, no change of look direction, speed or sprint, no flicker of light or blocks, no sound cut, no fall or bump. The two places must look identical around the player at the moment of the move, the move must keep the player's exact offset, look and velocity (relative teleport flags, and client-side handling if the server move alone shows a hitch), and it must work while sprinting, jumping, going up and going down, and for two players each looping by themselves. Mobs, dropped items and a tamed Pochita following the player: say what happens to them and make it not look broken.
3. Document for the next job (no fixing): walk from the door to the start of the boss fight and through the fight's arena, and list every place that looks bad, especially where blocks meet the devil's entity/model (gaps, z-fighting, flat colour, different pixel sizes, lighting that doesn't match, the checkerboard walls). One still per problem.

Tests: a game test that walks the stair routes geometrically (every step on each route has a walkable rise and two free blocks above) for every variant/rotation; a game test that the loop move keeps offset, yaw, pitch and velocity within a tiny tolerance and works for two players; and a filmed self-test that really holds the forward key up and down the stairs and round the loop three times and fails if the player ends up in the wrong place or broke a block. All game tests must pass after your last edit: `./gradlew runGametest` from the repo root; count in `build/gametest/junit.xml` (it is one line: `grep -o '<testcase' | wc -l` and `grep -o '<failure' | wc -l`). 283 on your base. Known flaky, rerun once before investigating: `each_fighter_gets_the_whole_drop`, two form tests, a Beam missile test.

The film (real play: first person, HUD on, normal FOV, no grade, no cuts): the player walks in the front door, up the stairs, round the loop three times without stopping, and back down. 1080x1920. Follow how the existing hotel self-test films (`./gradlew runSelftest -Pselftest=hotel -Ptarget=<yours> -Pusername=Kortev -Pcapture=true -Pwidth=1080 -Pheight=1920 "-Pjvmargs=-Xmx2600m -XX:TieredStopAtLevel=1"`). Copy to the main checkout `C:\Users\gamek\Documents\GitHub\loft\work\hotel\walk\`: `walk.mp4`, `walk_sheet.jpg` (a contact sheet, at most 1000 px wide, with the frames around each loop move side by side so a snap would show), and `looks_bad.jpg` (the stills from part 3 as one sheet, at most 1000 px wide, numbered to match your list). Keep the raw captures there under `raw/`. Watch your own sheets before finishing: if you can see where the loop happens, it isn't done.
