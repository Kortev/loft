# 2.2a The first night: the Tomato Devil comes for you

Branch `first-night`, cut from `claude/chainsaw-man` (`git checkout -b first-night claude/chainsaw-man`).
Size M. Lane: `devil/` (and its client side). Read this brief and the code; nothing else in `plan/`.

## Why (Fable's pick, 2026-11-11; changed the same day)

kortev likes the Tomato Devil's look and its teaser ("looks awesome"), but it only ever popped in like any mob. The
first plan was a scripted "second dusk" visit per player; kortev said a timed, scripted arrival doesn't feel right.
The valuable part is the arrival itself, so that is now simply how Tomato Devils turn up at night, by chance.
kortev's rules that apply: devils are creepy (look, behaviour, sound); design for someone who just plays; every
attack has a tell; synthesised sounds only.

## What happens

1. **The arrival is its spawn.** The Tomato is out of the monsters' spawn list; a small server-side spawner (like
   vanilla's phantoms or patrols) brings it. Only in the overworld, at night by the clock, not on Peaceful, only with
   `doMobSpawning` on, and not before the world's second night.
2. **The chance.** Each survival/adventure player under open sky gets rolls spread through the night, so a night
   brings an arrival about 40% of the time (one named constant). After an arrival, every player within 128 blocks
   of it gets none that night or the next 3 nights (`QUIET_NIGHTS = 3`, saved on the player). None if a Tomato Devil,
   its seeds or another arrival is within 128 blocks of the spot (so players together share one), and none at a
   player in a lair (the hotel), riding, or asleep (or with a sleeper within 24 blocks of the spot).
   `/summon` and the spawn egg still make one at once, with no arrival and no quiet spell.
3. **The arrival (about 8 s, the part worth filming).**
   - 0 s: the music stops and a quiet "air drops out" sound plays for players within about 32 blocks (new
     synthesised sound). Nothing else yet.
   - 2 s: 14-18 blocks away, on solid ground under the sky, in front of the player where a spot exists (else any
     side), eyes open in the dark low to the ground: the Tomato's own eyeshine, looking at the player. A wet
     breath comes from there, so a player facing away turns round.
   - 4 s: the ground there bulges: block-crack particles of that block, the swelling tomato from the comeback
     (reuse it, faster), dirt thrown up.
   - 7 s: it bursts and the devil stands there. Every eye snaps to the nearest player (the existing notice), then
     the fight starts as normal. No block is changed; no damage is possible before the burst.
4. **Every Tomato Devil** (no special mark): villagers flee it and it doesn't hurt them; iron golems ignore it.
   Despawning stays as it was.
5. (Dropped: the per-player "second dusk", the "first night" mark and the `firstNightDevil` rule.)
6. (Dropped.)
7. **Advancement.** If the mod has an advancements tab, add "Devils are real" for killing your first devil. If
   there's no tab yet, skip it and say so.
8. Not now: any tooltip or line about Public Safety (the office doesn't exist yet).

## What was built

Built to the changed design (no per-player second dusk, no "first night" mark, no `firstNightDevil` rule; none of
those was ever built).

- **Spawner** `devil/TomatoArrivals`: rolls every 200 ticks per player; `NIGHT_CHANCE = 0.4` gives the per-roll chance
  (about 1% a roll over the 50 rolls of a 13000-23000 night). Rules: overworld, night by the clock, time of day
  at least 24000 (second night on), not Peaceful, `doMobSpawning` on, survival/adventure, alive, not riding or
  asleep, open sky (heightmap, leaves don't count), not inside a hotel's box, no sleeper within 24 blocks of the
  spot, no Tomato Devil / seed / arrival within 128 blocks of the spot. Spot: 14-18 blocks, solid non-bedrock
  ground at the heightmap, within 8 blocks up or down, room for the devil's box; 24 tries within 50 degrees of the
  look, then 24 any side. `arrive(player)` is the forced-roll hook (tests and film).
- **Quiet spell**: `QUIET_NIGHTS = 3`. Every player within 128 blocks of an arrival gets a saved attachment
  (`chainsawman:tomato_quiet_until`, copied on death) = tonight's night number + 3; nights run dusk to dusk, so
  nothing that night or the next three, and the fourth night it may come again. Spawn eggs and `/summon` start
  none. This also gives the "at most one a night".
- **Arrival** `devil/TomatoArrivalEntity` (saved, can't be hit or damaged, no collision): 0 s the music is stopped
  (a stop-sound packet for the music category) and the new synthesised `tomato.hush` plays for players within 32
  blocks; 2 s eyes open one after another, a wet breath and a click from the spot; 4 s the swell sounds, block-crack
  particles of the ground block and dirt clods thrown up, growing; 7 s burst (juice, pulp, dirt), the devil stands
  there facing the nearest player with `notice` set (every eye snaps, the 24-tick stand still). No block changed.
  Client `TomatoArrivalRenderer`: the body sunk with its crown at ground level, the devil's own eyes drawn 1.6x,
  brighter, with a pale additive halo each so they read at 15 blocks at night; the body swells and rises from 4 s.
- **Every Tomato**: out of the biome spawn list (and its spawn restriction removed); villagers see it as danger
  within 12 blocks (mixin on the villagers' hostile sensor) and its slap, roll, hop and spit pass over villagers
  and iron golems; iron golems never target it (mixin on `IronGolemEntity.canTarget`). Despawning unchanged.
- **Sound** `tools/gen_chainsaw_sounds.py` `tomato_hush` (3 s: the hiss closing down to nothing, a low pressure
  swell, a faint ring, one wet shift far off).
- **Advancement**: skipped; the mod has no advancements tab yet.
- **Tests** (TomatoGameTests): the eight from the list, all with forced rolls; `it_spawns_only_at_night_on_the_surface`
  replaced by `it_comes_only_by_its_arrival` (in no monster list; a hand-placed devil starts no quiet spell).
  Full suite: 291 passed (283 - 1 + 9).
- **Film** `-Ptarget=firstnight` (`TomatoFirstNight`): stone sword and torches by a dirt hut, second night, clock
  running; the roll forced 3 s in, the rest played through the real keys. 66.8 s (arrival 3-10 s, fight 10-55 s,
  three seeds burned and two cut up). In `work/tomato/first_night.mp4`, sheet `first_night_sheet.jpg`, raw takes in
  `work/tomato/first_night_raw/`.
