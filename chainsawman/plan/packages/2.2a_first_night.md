# 2.2a The first night: the Tomato Devil comes for you

Branch `first-night`, cut from `claude/chainsaw-man` (`git checkout -b first-night claude/chainsaw-man`).
Size M. Lane: `devil/` (and its client side). Read this brief and the code; nothing else in `plan/`.

## Why (Fable's pick, 2026-11-11)

kortev likes the Tomato Devil's look and its teaser ("looks awesome"). But a player who installs the mod and just
plays meets it only by luck (spawn weight 2). The plan's first hour says: at your second dusk, wherever you are, a
weak devil comes for you, staged big. This builds that, with no office, licence or board needed.
kortev's rules that apply: devils are creepy (look, behaviour, sound); design for someone who just plays; every
attack has a tell; synthesised sounds only.

## What happens

1. **When.** Once per player, at dusk of their second day in the world (counted from the player's own first join,
   so late joiners get theirs). Not on Peaceful, not in spectator/creative, only in the overworld.
2. **It can't be missed, and it's never unfair.** It needs the player under open sky. If they're underground or
   indoors at dusk, it waits and fires the first moment in that night, or any later night, that they stand under
   the sky. Dusk comes before a bed works, so sleeping doesn't skip it. Never while the player is in a lair (the
   hotel), riding, or already fighting a Tomato Devil.
3. **The arrival (about 8 s, the part worth filming).**
   - 0 s: the music stops and a quiet "air drops out" sound plays for that player (new synthesised sound). Nothing
     else yet.
   - 2 s: 14-18 blocks away, on solid ground under the sky, in front of the player where a spot exists (else any
     side), eyes open in the dark low to the ground: the Tomato's own eyeshine, looking at the player. A wet
     breath comes from there, so a player facing away turns round.
   - 4 s: the ground there bulges: block-crack particles of that block, the swelling tomato from the comeback
     (reuse it, faster), dirt thrown up.
   - 7 s: it bursts and the devil stands there. Every eye snaps to the player (the existing notice), then the
     fight starts as normal. No block is changed.
4. **The devil is the normal Tomato Devil** with one mark ("first night"): it never despawns, it only hunts the
   player it came for (and whoever hits it), villagers flee it but it doesn't hurt them, iron golems ignore it.
   If the existing devil already does some of this, keep it.
5. **Players together share one.** Everyone within 48 blocks who hasn't had theirs is marked done by the same
   arrival. Dying to it doesn't bring another; the devil stays where it is (as now).
6. **Game rule** `firstNightDevil`, default on, beside the mod's other rules.
7. **Advancement.** If the mod has an advancements tab, add "Devils are real" for killing your first devil. If
   there's no tab yet, skip it and say so.
8. Not now: any tooltip or line about Public Safety (the office doesn't exist yet).
