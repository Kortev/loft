## 1. The model (Fable checked it against the wiki's anime picture)

The arms and the walk are good: keep them. The body is not the show's yet. Look at the Tomato Devil page on the
Chainsaw Man wiki (anime picture) before starting; don't copy its files into the repo.

- **Colour and shape.** Ours is a clean ball in flat bright red. The show's is a deep dark red, lumpy, wider than
  tall, with lobes running top to bottom like a beefsteak tomato: dark in the creases, lighter on the bulges.
- **Eyes.** Ours: 26 small ones that read as white rings round black holes. The show's: about a dozen, each BIG,
  a bulging white eyeball on a mound of flesh, with a red-brown iris and a dark pupil. They must read as eyes
  from 10 blocks. They still follow you, blink (fleshy lids) and burst when it's hurt.
- **Mouth.** Ours is a thin slit. The show's is the centre of the body: most of its height, thick lips, long
  rows of teeth that show even when it's shut. It opens wide for the spit.
- Hands 1.3 times bigger, arms a little thicker.
- Deliver `work/tomato/model.jpg` (at most 900 px): front, side and three-quarter, from 4 and from 10 blocks.

## 2. The fight (too easy up close, and it repeats)

Today you stand in front, step out of each slap and hit in between: 15 s with a sword, and slap/spit loops.

- **New move, the hop,** for anyone who hugs it. A player within 3 blocks for 2 s: it squats low on all eight
  arms (tell 14 ticks or more), springs up and lands. 3 damage and everyone within 3.5 blocks is thrown back
  about 5 blocks. Backing out during the squat avoids it. That throw puts you in roll range, so the roll follows
  when it can: hop, roll, bait it into a tree, punish. That's the fight's rhythm.
- No move more than twice running.
- **Length.** A scripted player, first sight to kill: iron sword 25-35 s, stone 30-40 s, the saws 10-14 s. Get
  there with the rhythm first, then health (never above 90). Report the three numbers.
- **The spit** must be dodged by a sidestep at walking speed from 5 blocks or more. Round 1's film failed it
  twice. Find out why and fix it (wider gaps or a longer tell).
- **Dying on its back** looks odd. Make it on purpose: the arms fold in tight over the body like a dead spider's,
  then it swells. Upright, it sinks and the arms curl under.
- **The comeback** has 60% health and is a real fight; don't stage its health in the film.

## 3. The burst

Cluttered red squares today. Make it one shape you can read: the skin splits into 6 to 8 big chunks that fly, land
and fade; one ring of juice on the ground; a third of today's small particles. Each seed arcs out with a short
bright trail so you see where it lands, and seeds in the ground glow stronger and pulse (findable in grass at night).

## 4. The seeds: fire, or a chainsaw

Fable's reading of kortev's worry: a torch shouldn't be the only handy answer, and a player without one is stuck
watching it come back. Fire stays the show's rule and the line stays "Burn them." Ways to end a seed:

- **Fire of any kind,** as built, torch included (still used up).
- **Burning is a moment:** the seed catches, flares for about a second with a shriek, and shrivels to ash.
- **Pochita eats them.** Set down within 16 blocks of a seed, he runs to the nearest one and chews it up (about
  30 ticks: teeth whirring, pulp flying), then goes for the next. Devils eat devils; no XP for it.
- **A chainsaw shreds them.** One hit from Pochita held as a chainsaw, or from Chainsaw Man's saws.
- A sword or a fist doesn't: a squelch, and the seed shudders.
- Nobody is ever stuck: a line still dies out by itself (one seed fewer each comeback), as built.

## 5. The film

Played, first person, HUD on, normal FOV, dusk, about 75 s, under 10 MB, 540x960. Starts with 5 s of it walking at
you so the gait shows. Then: dodge the slap, hug it and get hopped, the roll that follows hits you, bait the next
roll into a tree and punish, sidestep the spit, kill it, the burst. Seeds: two by torch, Pochita (set down
beforehand) eats two, one missed on purpose. The comeback fought for real, killed, its seeds ended. Only the
missed seed's minute may be skipped.

Don't touch the hotel code, `Squad.java` or `ShowcaseSelfTest.java`.
