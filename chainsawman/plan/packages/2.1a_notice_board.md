# 2.1a The village notice board, the licence, and selling a devil's part

Branch `notice-board`, cut from `claude/chainsaw-man` (`git checkout -b notice-board claude/chainsaw-man`).
Size M. New lane: `safety/` (main and client), registering its own blocks, items and sounds. Read this brief and
the code; nothing else in `plan/`.

## Why (kortev said "Yes, start it", 2026-11-11)

Today, after a player kills a devil, nothing follows: the carcass does nothing. This is the first piece of the
loop: a board in every village, a hunter's licence, and yen for a devil's part. No office, captain, missions or
shop yet (those come next; say plainly in the report that yen buys nothing yet).
Rules that apply: design for someone who installs and just plays; one player first, works with a few friends
(everything per player, nothing against strangers); nothing scripted or forced; synthesised sounds only; the
show's world (Public Safety, Japan in 1997), not its story.

## What to build

1. **The notice board.** Every village gets one near its bell: a small wooden board (vanilla planks/fences)
   holding two new blocks: the **poster** and the **phone**. It is put up by the mod when a village's bell first
   comes within about 64 blocks of a player and that village has none (so old worlds get it too, and it's there
   before the player sees the spot). Flat free ground within about 6 blocks of the bell; replace only air and
   plants; never inside a house or on a path block if another spot exists. One per village, remembered in the
   world's saved data. Breaking it is allowed (it's the player's world); it is not rebuilt.
2. **The poster** (block). Reads "DEVIL HUNTERS WANTED / PUBLIC SAFETY": white paper, red header, black print.
   Using it gives the player a **hunter's licence** if they don't hold one (paper sound, and one action-bar line:
   "Public Safety is hiring devil hunters."). Using it again while holding one does nothing but the sound.
3. **The licence** (item, stack of 1). Using it, or pressing a new key (default J, if free), opens the licence
   screen. The data is saved on the player, not in the item: lose the card and the poster gives another.
   The screen is one card, in the Public Safety look (black, white, one red line): the player's face and name,
   "Public Safety Devil Hunter", rank "4th Class", yen, **devils hunted** (each kind with a count), and
   **contracts** (the ones the mod already has, with their price, read from the existing contract code). Show
   nothing that doesn't exist yet (no partner, missions or power).
4. **The phone** (block): a green 1990s Japanese public phone on the board [Fable's pick; colour is a guess].
   Using it sells every devil part in the player's inventory for yen. The payday must be a moment: it rings,
   a voice-like murmur (synthesised), a cash/envelope sound, and the yen counts up on screen over about 2 s.
   Tomato carcass: 3,000 yen. The first part a player ever sells adds 10,000 once. With no parts: one short
   line, "Nothing to report.", and a dial tone. No licence yet: the phone gives one first (same as the poster).
5. **Devils hunted.** When a devil dies and a player dealt the killing blow (or most of the damage, if the mod
   already tracks that), that player's count for that kind goes up, licence or not. Do this with a server death
   event and an entity-type tag `chainsawman:devils` (add the Tomato Devil to it), not by editing the Tomato's
   classes: another builder is changing those right now on another branch.
6. **The carcass tooltip:** "Public Safety pays for these. Look for the notice board in a village."
7. Per player always: two friends each have their own licence, yen and counts. No paying each other yet.

## What was built

New lane `safety/` (main: `Safety`, `NoticeBoards`, `SafetyLedger`, `BoardBlock`, `LicenceItem`, `SafetyPayloads`; client:
`SafetyClient`, `LicenceScreen`, `PaydayHud`). One line each added to the mod's init and client init, one block each in
`en_us.json` and `sounds.json`. Textures by `tools/gen_safety_textures.py`, sounds by `tools/gen_safety_sounds.py`.

- **Board.** Every 2 s each player's surroundings (64 blocks, loaded chunks) are searched for bells by the blocks themselves
  (each chunk section's palette first, so it is cheap). Not by points of interest: a village generated a moment ago has no
  bell point of interest yet (seen in the film's fresh world). A bell counts only inside a vanilla village structure (never
  a player's own bell and trading hall), and never in or by one of the mod's hotels (a loaded home's box plus a board's
  reach, or the hotel structure). A bell turned down is left alone for a minute (in memory, not saved). Tests mark their
  bells as villages through `NoticeBoards.TEST_VILLAGE_BELLS`. The phone hangs at eye level. Board: 4 wide, spruce fence posts at both ends, two rows of spruce planks, a slab roof; the
  poster's paper (drawn 1.75 x 1.75 blocks, 192 px texture) on the left half, the green phone on the right at chest height.
  Spot: within 6 blocks of the bell, flat, open to the sky (heightmap, so not under a roof or a tree), on solid full ground,
  replacing only air and plants, facing the bell about 4 blocks off; a dirt path only if nothing else fits (scored, not
  forbidden). One per village: bells within 48 blocks of a done bell are the same village; remembered in world data
  (`chainsawman_safety`) even when there was no room or the board is broken, so it is never put up again.
- **Poster / licence / phone** as the brief says. Licence: stack of 1, opens the card on use or J. Card: black, white rim,
  one red line; face (skin), name, "Public Safety Devil Hunter", "Rank: 4th Class", yen, devils hunted (each kind x count, or
  "None yet"), contracts (each held id with its price lines, reused from the contract screen's lang keys; "None"). Does not
  pause the game. Phone: sells every Tomato carcass (3,000 each), +10,000 on a player's first sale ever; server plays the
  ring (handset up, Japanese ringback, answer click, a muffled voice), client shows "Reporting to Public Safety..." then at
  2.7 s the cash sound, the lines and the total counting up over 2 s with clicks, a pop and red flash as it lands. No parts:
  dial tone and "Nothing to report.". No licence: it gives one first, then sells.
- **Devils hunted:** `AFTER_DEATH`, entity in `chainsawman:devils` (the Tomato was already in it, so no tag edit), the
  killing player (or the last player who hit it). Per player attachments (yen, hunted, first-report flag), kept through
  death. Yen buys nothing yet.
- **Tooltip** on anything Public Safety pays for (the carcass), from the client lane.
- **Tests** `SafetyGameTests` (6). **Film** `SafetySelfTest` (`-Pselftest=safety`), the nearest village to the hotel's
  plains in the self-test world; needs a clean copy of the world each take (delete `build/selftest/saves/selftest`).
  Staged: one Tomato already counted (the carcass's), the start 22 blocks in front of the board, the pauses.
- **Results:** 289 game tests, all passing on the last run (the Beam test `sprintdivesandlettinggosurfaces` failed in two
  earlier runs and passed in the third; a hotel test timed out once). Film 28.7 s, 1080x1920, in `work/safety/`
  (`board.mp4`, `board_sheet.jpg`, `licence.jpg`; raw takes in `work/safety/raw/`).
- **Not seen yet:** the card with a contract held (the film's player holds none); a board in desert, snow, savanna or taiga
  villages (only a plains village filmed); several friends at one board on a real server.
