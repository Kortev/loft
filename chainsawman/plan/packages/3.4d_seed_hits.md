# 3.4d Tomato seeds: cutting them up works too

Branch `tomato-seed-hits`, cut from `claude/chainsaw-man`. Small: one entity, one lang line, tests. No film.

## What kortev said (2026-11-10)

"Is there a way to destroy the [seeds] without flames or Pochita? Because again, I don't think a lot of people
are going to have Pochita." (Second time they've asked for a way that isn't fire.)

## Fable's pick

A player who sees glowing things in the ground hits them. So that works, slowly, and fire stays the smart way.

- **Three hits end a seed.** Any melee hit from a player, with anything, fist included. Hit one: a squelch, juice,
  the seed shudders and shrinks a little. Hit two: it splits open and shrinks more. Hit three: it pops (a small
  shriek, pulp) and is gone. Cuts don't heal. A player's arrow or other projectile counts as a hit too.
- Unchanged: fire of any kind and a torch end it at once (the flare), a chainsaw shreds it in one, Pochita eats it.
- The swelling seed (the 10 s before a comeback) can be cut up the same way, and that stops the comeback.
- The action-bar line becomes: "Its seeds are in the ground. Burn them or cut them up."
- Five seeds is fifteen swings in the minute you have: doable, but burning is quicker. That's the point.

## What was built

- `TomatoSeedEntity`: a synced, saved cut count (`cuts()`, NBT "Cuts"). Any hit whose attacker is a player (fist, sword,
  their arrow) calls `cut()`: squelch, juice and a shudder; from the second, pulp too; the third pops it (small high
  shriek, squelch, juice and pulp) and it is gone. Works on a swelling seed, which then never hatches. Fire, torch,
  chainsaw and Pochita unchanged (fire and saws are checked first). Non-player hits still only shudder it.
- `TomatoSeedRenderer`: one cut draws it at 80%; two cuts draw it at 60% as two halves splayed apart, redder inside.
  A swelling tomato shrinks by the same factor.
- Action bar: "Its seeds are in the ground. Burn them or cut them up."
- Tests: `three_hits_end_a_seed` (sword, fist, arrow; cuts survive a save) and
  `cutting_the_swelling_seed_stops_the_comeback` replace `a_sword_does_not_end_a_seed`.
