# 3.3b The Leech Devil, round 2: the body, a reach fighter, the film at night

Branch `leech-round2`, cut from `claude/chainsaw-man` after round 1 was merged (308 tests green). The planner's notes on
round 1, saved as given.

## What reads well

The face. The grin of blocky teeth under hanging black hair, no eyes, is creepy at a glance. Keep it.

## What is weak (from round 1's own sheets)

1. **The body** reads as a smooth dark lump on thin pole legs. It should read from the side, at 10 blocks, as the fact
   sheet's creature: a big, sagging, deeply wrinkled body (heavy folds and rings you can see as shapes, not texture; the
   belly hanging low between the legs), thick legs with a clear knee, planted wide, and tentacle arms that are thick at the
   shoulder. The sucker mouths are the creature's signature and are too small: make them about twice the size, the pale
   lip and dark red inside readable from across a field, on all six limbs.
2. **The face fills the frame** nearly the whole fight film: the body, arms and attacks are hardly seen. It is a reach
   fighter: it holds its ground at about 6-8 blocks, circling, and backs off to that range after each move. The player's
   damage comes mainly from cutting what reaches out to them: the tongue while it is held out, the latched or stunned arm
   lying near them, and the body only in the skid after a trample. Hits on a limb part pass to the devil (keep 1.5x on the
   tongue; give the stunned arm the same). The hide's armour stays, so walking up and hacking the body is slow, and
   cutting limbs is the fight. Keep every tell and number otherwise; winnable with an iron sword in 60-90 s; say what a
   stone sword measures.
3. **The tongue must be reliably cuttable by a person**: the part of the held-out tongue nearest the player (its last 3
   blocks toward them) is the hit area, forgiving of aim. Film one cut on it.
4. **Sound**: no shriek on every hit. Shriek only when an arm is torn loose or the tongue is cut; ordinary hits get a
   short wet grunt (three variants, synthesised), never twice within 15 ticks.
5. **The film**: at dusk going into night, not daylight. Real play, HUD on, normal FOV, no grade. The whole creature in
   view in most frames (check the contact sheet). Replace `work\leech\fight.mp4`, `contact_sheet.jpg`, `model.jpg` (round
   1's to `work\leech\raw\round1\`).
6. **Reels**: three extra takes at full quality under `work\leech\raw\reel_takes\`, one sheet
   `work\leech\reel_takes_sheet.jpg` (at most 1000 px wide): (a) at night, it walks toward you from far off, humming, and
   the face turns to you; (b) the latch from the player's view: the sucker whips on, red at the edges, three cuts, it
   drops; (c) the tongue lancing past the lens as you sidestep, then the trample and skid.

## What was built

Built on `leech-round2` (2026-11-11), merged with `claude/chainsaw-man` (devils grow, hotel walk) before the last test run.

- **The body** (`tools/leech_devil_3d.py`): the torso is now a chain of eight fat rolls barely smoothed together, so the
  creases between them are deep shapes, not paint. The belly sags lowest in the middle (0.36 above the ground) between the
  legs, and loose folds of skin hang off each roll's lower sides. The legs are thick (thigh 0.43 at the hip) with a big knee
  knob, planted wide (feet 1.8-1.9 out from the middle). The tentacles are drawn twice as thick at the shoulder as at the
  tip, off bigger shoulders. Every sucker is 1.9x round 1's, on all six limbs. The face and hair are unchanged.
- **A reach fighter** (`LeechDevilEntity`): it keeps you about 7 blocks off, circling sideways with its face on you, and
  backs off to that range after each move (and fast if you come within 5). On first sight its whole body slowly turns to
  you. The tongue and the latch reach from there; the trample still only from 8+.
  - The torn-loose arm now lies on the ground out to where it held you, for 70 of its 120 stunned ticks, and a cut on it
    counts 1.5x (as on the tongue).
  - The held-out tongue's hit area is two 1.6-block boxes over the 3 blocks of it nearest you, following you as you move.
  - The latched arm's cuttable box sits 2.4 blocks up the arm.
  - The hide's armour (14) stays, so the body is slow to hack. The fighter's own logs show the damage comes from limbs:
    in the film, 18 cuts on the lying arm, 6 on the tongue and 9 in skids, against 3 on the body otherwise.
  - Every tell and attack number from round 1 is unchanged; the trample's hit width is now its body and planted feet (1.7).
- **Sound**: ordinary hits get one of three synthesised wet grunts, never twice within 15 ticks. The shriek only plays when
  an arm is torn loose or the tongue is cut.
- **Tests**: 7 Leech tests; the cut test now also checks that the stunned arm lies within reach and takes 1.5x.
- **Film** (`-Ptarget=played`): starts at time 12300 with the daylight cycle on, so it runs from sunset into night.
  `work/leech/fight.mp4` is 76.5 s and 16.5 MB, so there's no phone copy. The devil is killed at 68.7 s; all 3 tramples
  are dodged, the tongue is cut 6 times, and 5 latches are cut loose. On the contact sheet about 15 of 24 frames show the
  whole creature. Round 1's files are in `raw/round1/`; earlier round 2 takes and the final capture are in `raw/round2/`.
- **Reel takes** (`-Ptarget=reel-walk`, `reel-latch`, `reel-tongue`): in `raw/reel_takes/` (capture folders at full quality
  plus muxed mp4s), with `reel_takes_sheet.jpg`.
- **Measured duels** (scripted fighter): iron sword 63.4 s, stone sword 85.1 s.
