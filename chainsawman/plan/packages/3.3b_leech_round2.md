# 3.3b The Leech Devil, round 2: the body, a reach fighter, the film at night

Branch `leech-round2`, cut from `claude/chainsaw-man` after round 1 was merged (308 tests green). The planner's notes on
round 1, saved as given.

## What reads well

The face. The grin of blocky teeth under hanging black hair, no eyes, is creepy at a glance. Keep it.

## What is weak (from round 1's own sheets)

1. **The body** reads as a smooth dark lump on thin pole legs. It should read from the side, at 10 blocks, as the fact
   sheet's creature: a big, sagging, deeply wrinkled body (heavy folds and rings you can see as shapes, not texture; the
   belly hanging low between the legs), thick legs with a clear knee, planted wide, and tentacle arms that are thick at the
   shoulder. The sucker mouths are the creature's signature and are too small: make them about twice the size, the pale
   lip and dark red inside readable from across a field, on all six limbs.
2. **The face fills the frame** nearly the whole fight film: the body, arms and attacks are hardly seen. It is a reach
   fighter: it holds its ground at about 6-8 blocks, circling, and backs off to that range after each move. The player's
   damage comes mainly from cutting what reaches out to them: the tongue while it is held out, the latched or stunned arm
   lying near them, and the body only in the skid after a trample. Hits on a limb part pass to the devil (keep 1.5x on the
   tongue; give the stunned arm the same). The hide's armour stays, so walking up and hacking the body is slow, and
   cutting limbs is the fight. Keep every tell and number otherwise; winnable with an iron sword in 60-90 s; say what a
   stone sword measures.
3. **The tongue must be reliably cuttable by a person**: the part of the held-out tongue nearest the player (its last 3
   blocks toward them) is the hit area, forgiving of aim. Film one cut on it.
4. **Sound**: no shriek on every hit. Shriek only when an arm is torn loose or the tongue is cut; ordinary hits get a
   short wet grunt (three variants, synthesised), never twice within 15 ticks.
5. **The film**: at dusk going into night, not daylight. Real play, HUD on, normal FOV, no grade. The whole creature in
   view in most frames (check the contact sheet). Replace `work\leech\fight.mp4`, `contact_sheet.jpg`, `model.jpg` (round
   1's to `work\leech\raw\round1\`).
6. **Reels**: three extra takes at full quality under `work\leech\raw\reel_takes\`, one sheet
   `work\leech\reel_takes_sheet.jpg` (at most 1000 px wide): (a) at night, it walks toward you from far off, humming, and
   the face turns to you; (b) the latch from the player's view: the sucker whips on, red at the edges, three cuts, it
   drops; (c) the tongue lancing past the lens as you sidestep, then the trample and skid.
