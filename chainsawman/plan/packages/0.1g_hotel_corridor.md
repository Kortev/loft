## What to build

1. **Down the stairs inside the loop.** Today, while the loop holds you, going down the stairs snaps you to the
   corridor's start with your view reset. In the show every way out leads back to the same floor. Make it so:
   going down (or up) the stairwell from the looping floor brings you out on the same floor, with the move as
   invisible as the corridor's (offset, look, speed kept; the two places identical around you at that moment).
2. **The one-block offset.** At hotel turns 1-3, `HotelBuilder`'s point coordinates sit one block off its block
   coordinates (`Shaft.at` does it right). Fix it at the root, check every hotel turn with tests, and say what
   moved. Don't change `Shaft` or the arena (another builder is rebuilding the arena on branch `eternity-arena`).
3. **One pixel size.** Everything the mod draws in the hotel uses texels 1/16 of a block, like Minecraft's own
   pixels, shaded by texture, not smooth blobs:
   - The eyes in the wallpaper: pixel-art eyes set into the wall (a lid, a socket, a shadow), not smooth white
     discs stuck on it. They still open as laps go on and follow the player.
   - The flesh lumps: grow out of the wall or floor through a root that joins the surface, same pixel size,
     wet highlights by texture. No lump floats clear of the wall.
   - Where the devil tears out of the corridor's end: the wall round it is torn (broken wallpaper, lath, flesh
     pushing through), so model and blocks join. Teeth sit in gums and don't poke through blocks.
4. **The stairwell** is a bare grey box. Dress it as the hotel's stairwell: the corridor's wallpaper or plain
   painted walls with a skirting, a handrail, a light on each landing, and a floor sign on each landing. On the
   looping floor the sign always reads 8.
5. **8:18.** The show's clocks all stop at 8:18 inside the loop. Put a wall clock (our own small model or a
   pixel-art block) at the corridor's ends and in the lobby; outside the loop it shows the real time of day,
   inside the loop it reads 8:18 and its second hand twitches back.
6. Creepy stays the aim: the eyes open one more each lap; a survivor's whisper repeats; nothing jumps out.

## What was built

Film: `work/hotel/corridor/corridor.mp4` (68 s, 1080x1920, game sound mixed, real keys, first person, HUD on, FOV 70, one take;
`corridor_phone.mp4` 25 MB), `corridor_sheet.jpg` (six frames round each of the three corridor moves and the two stair moves),
`corridor_look.jpg` (before: looks_bad 1, 2, 3, 10; after: the same things, and the clock), raw capture, sounds and stills in `raw/`.
Take: `-Ptarget=corridor` (`HotelCorridorWalk`); close stills: `-Ptarget=corridorlook`.

1. Stairs in the loop. The well is now one unit repeated every storey, with a **spine wall** between the two flights (it replaces the
   iron bars): no storey sees past the next one up or down. While the loop holds, the stairwell has a window like the corridor's (feet
   25.25 to 29.25, local): walk down past the floor below's landing and you are put up a storey; walk up past the loop floor's first step and
   you are put down one. The move is the corridor's: relative on every axis and the look (`Hotel.shiftUp`), followers in the well carried
   (`carryWell`, and `ClientLoop.beforeMove` moves them on the client first). To have something identical above 8 there is a **stair house
   on the roof** (x 12-18, z 0-4, y 33-38, `HotelPlan.SY` 36 -> 39) with the next flight and a shut door. While the loop holds, every floor
   sign in the well reads 8 and every well door shuts itself quietly when nobody is at it (`HotelBuilder.loopLook`, `shutWellDoors`).
   Going down twice in the film: two stair moves, look and place kept, out on 8 both times. Old worlds: `STAIRS` 1 -> 2 rebuilds the well
   with its walls and the stair house (x 12-18, z 0-4, ground to roof).
2. The offset. Cause: `HotelBuilder.world(origin, rot, double...)` turned points about the origin block's corner while blocks turn about its
   middle, so at turns 1-3 a point was a block off along each turned axis (an eye at a wall's face stood a block into the corridor; Hunt's
   restore of a cut block read the wrong plan cell). Fix: `HotelBuilder.at` / `localOf` (the blocks' frame, `Shaft.at`'s maths) and `dir`
   for directions. Moved to them: `Hotel`, `Hunt`, `Squad`, `ClientLoop`, `HotelStructure` (dir), the hotel tests. Kept on the old
   `world(double)` (marked as such): `Shaft`, `Arena`, `EternityDevilEntity`, `ArenaRenderer`, `HotelFight` (the arena's, rebuilt on
   `eternity-arena`: they should move to `at`/`localOf`/`dir`). What moved in play at turns 1-3: eyes, the breathing wall, flesh lumps,
   squad seats and exit, sounds, the corridor's things box, the seal's put-out point and the fight's put-in-front point.
   Test: every turn, points and blocks in the same place (and an eye at a wall's face on its wall block).
3. One pixel size (all drawn through `PixelArt`: squares a sixteenth of a block, one colour each, on the blocks' grid):
   - eyes in the wallpaper: pixel art (shadowed socket, lids that part a row at a time, veins, a pupil that moves a whole pixel to follow
     you, a seam with lashes when shut), lit by the corridor's light; eye places snapped to the grid. One more eye opens each lap (the nearest
     the devil's spot first). A whisper still repeats (unchanged).
   - flesh lumps: voxels (`FleshVoxels`), a heap in the floor-wall corner or a bulge on the wall, out of the surface with veins as roots over
     wall and floor; colours dark in folds, light on tops, a wet glint on some; grows a stage at a time; lit from the corridor side.
   - the walls bleeding toward the devil: new blocks, torn wallpaper (lath showing), torn lath (flesh between), wall flesh, deepest by the
     arena's edge; a bleeding cut splits the wallpaper on the flesh. Exit signs are a pixel-art block (was a lime block).
4. The stairwell: plastered walls (the corridor's sandstone) with a dark oak skirting on every floor it has, a wooden handrail with iron
   brackets on each flight (on the spine), a lantern hung over every half landing, a green floor sign on each landing (1 to 8; 8 in the loop).
5. Clocks: `hotel_clock` (a 12 px round face; the hands drawn by the client pixel by pixel). At the loop corridor's two ends, once a period
   on its back wall (x 7, 19, 31) and in the lobby. Time of day normally; 8:18 on the loop floor while the loop holds, the second hand ticking
   one on and snapping back.

Tests: `HotelCorridorGameTests` (3): the stair move down and up at turns 1 and 3 (place to 1e-6, look exact, speed, only a storey sent, all
relative, a pig carried, out on the loop floor's flight, signs reading 8); every turn's points and blocks together; clocks 8:18 only inside
the loop (lobby never), floor signs 8 only while it holds. `theLoopPutsYouBackSeamlesslyAtEveryTurn`'s stairs check now expects a storey up
