## What to build

1. **Down the stairs inside the loop.** Today, while the loop holds you, going down the stairs snaps you to the
   corridor's start with your view reset. In the show every way out leads back to the same floor. Make it so:
   going down (or up) the stairwell from the looping floor brings you out on the same floor, with the move as
   invisible as the corridor's (offset, look, speed kept; the two places identical around you at that moment).
2. **The one-block offset.** At hotel turns 1-3, `HotelBuilder`'s point coordinates sit one block off its block
   coordinates (`Shaft.at` does it right). Fix it at the root, check every hotel turn with tests, and say what
   moved. Don't change `Shaft` or the arena (another builder is rebuilding the arena on branch `eternity-arena`).
3. **One pixel size.** Everything the mod draws in the hotel uses texels 1/16 of a block, like Minecraft's own
   pixels, shaded by texture, not smooth blobs:
   - The eyes in the wallpaper: pixel-art eyes set into the wall (a lid, a socket, a shadow), not smooth white
     discs stuck on it. They still open as laps go on and follow the player.
   - The flesh lumps: grow out of the wall or floor through a root that joins the surface, same pixel size,
     wet highlights by texture. No lump floats clear of the wall.
   - Where the devil tears out of the corridor's end: the wall round it is torn (broken wallpaper, lath, flesh
     pushing through), so model and blocks join. Teeth sit in gums and don't poke through blocks.
4. **The stairwell** is a bare grey box. Dress it as the hotel's stairwell: the corridor's wallpaper or plain
   painted walls with a skirting, a handrail, a light on each landing, and a floor sign on each landing. On the
   looping floor the sign always reads 8.
5. **8:18.** The show's clocks all stop at 8:18 inside the loop. Put a wall clock (our own small model or a
   pixel-art block) at the corridor's ends and in the lobby; outside the loop it shows the real time of day,
   inside the loop it reads 8:18 and its second hand twitches back.
6. Creepy stays the aim: the eyes open one more each lap; a survivor's whisper repeats; nothing jumps out.
