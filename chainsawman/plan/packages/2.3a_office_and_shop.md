# 2.3a The Public Safety office, the captain and the shop (and the board's polish)

Branch `office-shop`, cut from `claude/chainsaw-man`. Size L. Lane: `safety/` (main and client). Read this
brief, "What was built" in `2.1a_notice_board.md`, and the code. Nothing else in `plan/`.

## Why

A player can now kill a devil, find the village notice board, get a licence and sell the carcass at the phone.
Then nothing: yen buys nothing. This is the next piece of the loop: a place to spend it, and the first thing
worth buying. kortev's rules: design for someone who installs and just plays; one player first, fine with a
few friends (everything per player); nothing scripted or forced; the show's world (Public Safety, Japan in
1997), not its story or its characters; synthesised sounds only; a power needs a look worth filming.

## What to build

1. **The office.** One small Public Safety branch office per village, at the village's edge. Raise it the same
   way and at the same moment as the notice board (when the village's bell first comes within 64 blocks of a
   player and the village has none; remembered in the same saved data; never rebuilt after it is broken):
   a flat spot within 48 blocks of the bell, on a foundation, never overlapping a building, a farm, a path or
   anything a player placed; if no spot fits, no office. The notice board's poster then says where it is
   ("Office: 40 m north-east" as a second action-bar line when you take the licence).
   The building, about 9 x 7, one storey and a flat roof: pale plaster walls, a dark tiled band, a sliding
   door, a sign over it reading PUBLIC SAFETY with a red mark, a lantern. Inside: a counter with the captain
   behind it, a desk with papers and a phone (it works like the board's phone), filing cabinets, a bench, a
   cork board (decor: no missions yet, so nothing on it claims to be one), a wall clock, and a **vending
   machine**. New blocks in the same pixel style as the poster and phone.
2. **The captain.** Our own character, not one from the show: a tired hunter in a black suit, white shirt,
   black tie, an old scar. Stands behind the counter, watches you, can't be hurt or pushed out, comes back if
   lost. Using them opens the shop.
3. **The shop** (a screen in the licence's style: black, white, one red line; your yen at the top):
   - **Public Safety katana**, 8,000 yen. A real sword (iron's damage plus one, iron's durability, can be
     enchanted and repaired with iron). **It makes devils bleed:** a devil cut by it bleeds for 8 s: dark blood
     runs and trails from the wound, it takes 1 damage a second, and it cannot heal in that time (the Leech's
     drinking heals nothing; a Tomato Devil that bursts while bleeding leaves two seeds fewer). Bleeding is the
     look worth filming: wounds that stay open on the model, a trail on the ground, a hiss.
   - **First-aid kit**, 800 yen: use it (1.5 s) to heal 8 over 4 s. Stack of 4.
   - **Blood pack**, 1,500 yen: for someone with the chainsaw heart it refills blood; for anyone else it does
     nothing and the tooltip says so. Stack of 4.
   - **Sells:** the captain buys devil parts at the phone's prices. Add the Leech's part, `leech_sucker`, at
     9,000 yen, at the phone too.
   - Buying is a moment: the captain slides the thing across, a stamp sound, the yen counts down.
4. **The vending machine.** Use it: 200 yen for a can of coffee (drink: heals 4, speed for 10 s). No yen: it
   rattles and gives nothing. A clunk and the can drops into your hand.
5. **The board's polish** (weak spots from 2.1a): the notice board becomes a proper one (posts, a small roof,
   a lantern, a few pinned papers beside the poster, the phone on its own post under the roof); a devil that
   came back from its seeds counts once on the licence, not twice; check and still the board and the office
   in plains, desert, savanna, snow and taiga villages.
