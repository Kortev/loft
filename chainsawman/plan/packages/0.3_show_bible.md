# 0.3 The show bible (first four sheets)

Branch `show-bible`, cut from `claude/chainsaw-man`. Documents only, no code.

## What this is for

Every design starts from checked show facts. Today they're scattered or from memory. The bible is one small fact
sheet per subject in `chainsawman/bible/`, so a brief can copy in exactly the facts it needs.

## Decided

- The mod uses the show's world (devils, contracts, looks), not its story. [kortev]
- **Nothing past the Reze arc** (manga chapter 52, the end of the Reze film). kortev hasn't seen further. Wiki pages
  mix in later events, later forms, later deaths and who someone "really" is. Leave all of that out, and don't
  hint at it ("later revealed...", "at this point"). If a fact can't be told without a later one, leave it out and
  write "left out: later canon" with no detail.
- A fact must be on the wiki **and** agree with a second source found by search, or be marked "one source".

## The four sheets, in this order

1. `fox_devil.md`: how it's called (the hand sign, the word), what appears, how big, colours, what it does to its
   target, what it's said to cost, who may contract it, and anything said about where or when it will or won't
   come.
2. `leech_devil.md`: look, size, how it moves and attacks, how it's beaten in the show, how it talks.
3. `tomato_devil.md`: look, size, where it's found, how it's killed, what happens to its body.
4. `hunters_gear.md`: what Public Safety hunters wear and carry (the suit, weapons seen in use), the office as a
   place (what the rooms look like), how pay, rank and partners are spoken of.

## The shape of a sheet (at most 4 KB each)

```
# <subject>
Checked on <date>. Covers up to chapter 52 only.

## Looks        (shape, size next to a person, colours, the parts you'd model first)
## Does         (what it's seen doing, in order, as staged in the show: where things are, who stands where)
## Rules        (what the show says about calling it, its price, its limits)
## Said         (short paraphrases of how it talks; no long quotes)
## Not known    (things a designer will want that the show doesn't say; say so plainly)
## Sources      (page names and searches used, one line each)
```

Each fact is one line. Mark each "wiki + search", "one source" or "seen in the anime/manga panel, chapter N".
Paraphrase; don't copy sentences from the wiki.

## How

- The Chainsaw Man wiki is the main source, plus a web search for each important fact. If fetching the wiki is
  blocked, try its `api.php?action=parse&page=<name>&prop=wikitext&format=json`, then search results. If nothing
  works, stop and say so; don't write from memory.
- Read `chainsawman/ETERNITY_ARC.md` once for the tone and level of detail. Don't read `HANDOFF.md`.
- Also write `chainsawman/bible/README.md`: a table of the sheets (file, subject, date), and the spoiler rule in
  three lines.

## The four checks

- Show: this *is* the show check. Staging matters as much as looks (where the thing is, where people stand).
- Minecraft, fun, other people: not for this package. Don't design anything.

## Done looks like

Four sheets and the README, committed by file name on `show-bible`, not pushed. Report short: for each sheet, how
many facts have two sources, what couldn't be found, and anything that contradicts
