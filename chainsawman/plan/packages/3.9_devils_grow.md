## Show facts (`chainsawman/bible/devil_strength.md`)

Stated in the show: a devil is as strong as its name is feared. Shown: blood heals a devil. Not in the show: a
devil growing just by being old. So here time counts only as fear spreading: a devil that stays near people is
talked about and feared more each night.

## The rule (Fable's design; numbers in named constants)

Each devil has a **growth** from 0 to 5, saved with it and synced to clients. One shared class (say
`devil/Growth`) holds the rule so the Leech Devil and others can use it later; only the Tomato uses it now.

It gains a step when:
- **A night ends and it lived through it near people**: a player or a villager was within 64 blocks at some
  point that night. One step a night at most from this.
- **A player died to it.** One step, at most once a night.
- **It came back from its seeds** (the player failed to finish it). One step.
Never more than two steps in one night, never above 5, and never while its chunk isn't loaded (a devil nobody
met is not waiting at full size). Growth is never lost.

What a step does:
- Health +10% of its base (so +50% at 5), healed by the gained amount. Damage +6% a step. Size +4% a step
  (model and hitbox). Its tells and timings do not change: it stays fair to dodge.
- **It looks worse each step**, readable at a glance side by side: more of its eyes open and they get bigger
  and redder-rimmed, the skin darkens and splits with wet seams, by step 4-5 veins pulse on it and it drips.
  Its breath and voice drop in pitch a little each step. Creepy, not just bigger.
- **Worth more:** one extra carcass at steps 2 and 4 (so up to three). A devil with growth 1 or more never
  despawns.
- When it gains a step while a player is within 32 blocks: a short shudder and a low swell of sound, so the
  player knows something changed. No text.

The arrival system stays as it is: a living Tomato Devil keeps new arrivals away within 128 blocks, so one left
alone grows where it stands.

`/summon` makes growth 0. Add a way to set it for testing and filming (an NBT field is enough, e.g. `Growth`).

## What was built

Built by Fable 5.2 (2026-11-11), branch `devils-grow`.

- **`devil/Growth`** (shared, any `MobEntity` can own one): level 0-5, the night counts (which night, steps in it, people
  near, a kill, ended), saved as NBT `Growth` (int) and `GrowthNight`. A night is dusk 13000 to dawn 23000 and counts with
  the day after it. Steps: the dark ends after a night with a player or villager within 64 blocks (looked at once a second,
  only in the dark, only while the devil ticks, so never while unloaded); a player killed by it (once a night, through
  `onKilledOther`, spit kills included); a comeback from its seeds. At most 2 a night, never above 5, never lost.
  A step sets persistent attribute modifiers: max health +10% of base (healed by the gain), `GENERIC_SCALE` +4% (model and
  hitbox), attack damage +6% (the moves multiply their own damage by `growth.damage()`), and from 1 the devil is persistent.
  Voice: `playSound` is overridden so every sound it makes is pitched 5% lower a step.
- **Tomato wiring**: tracked `GROWTH` and `GREW_AT`; slap, roll, hop and spit damage scaled; carcasses 1/2/3 (steps 2 and
  4); its seeds carry its growth NBT and the devil that climbs out reads it and takes a comeback step; hug and hop reach
  and the push use its real width. Gaining a step with a player within 32 blocks: `GREW_AT` is set (a 36-tick shudder and
  swell on the client, every eye wide) and the new sound `tomato.grow` (synthesised in `tools/gen_chainsaw_sounds.py`:
  low swell, throat groan, stretching skin, two heartbeats). Setting it by `/data merge entity ... {Growth:n}` on a devil
  already about shudders too; `/summon ... {Growth:5}` makes a full-health growth 5 with no shudder.
- **Look** (`client/devil/TomatoGrowthLook`, used by `TomatoDevilRenderer`): drawn scaled with its hands still planted
  where they stand in the world; skin darker and deeper red each step and flushing with a heartbeat at 4-5; arms greyer
  and bruised; 9 of 15 eyes open at 0 (the smallest stay shut, twitching under the lid) up to all 15 at 5; eyes 4.5% bigger
  a step; a raw red rim (the lids, tinted, swollen behind the ball) from step 1, growing; irises redden; 12 wet seams (the
  gash texture laid on the body's ellipsoid) splitting open 2/4/7/10/12 and lengthening; dark veins
  (`textures/entity/tomato_veins.png`, `tools/tomato_veins.py`) round the oldest seams from 3, pulsing at 4-5; red drips
  from the seams and lips at 4-5; the idle mouth hangs a little wider each step.
- **Tests** (`GrowthGameTests`, 9): night near people, night alone, death once a night (and the real kill hook), comeback
  (carried growth and from a plain seed), caps (5 and 2 a night), health/damage/size/voice per step, carcasses at 2 and 4,
  save/load plus `{Growth:5}` and the NBT shudder, no despawn.
- **Takes**: `TomatoSelfTest -Ptarget=growths` (the six stills, `TomatoGrownTakes`), `-Ptarget=grown` (the played duel
  against a growth 5 at night, iron sword, all five seeds torched, `TomatoFight`), `-Ptarget=growreel` (the reel take).
  Sheets by `tools/growth_sheet.py`. All in `work/tomato/grown/`.

## Round 2 (planner's notes, 2026-11-11)

The rule is right and stays. The look was not strong enough: on `growth_steps.jpg` steps 0-2 looked the same and 5
against 0 read as "a bit darker and bigger". Tints and +20% don't change the outline, and the outline is what reads at
phone size. So: size +8% a step (+40% at 5), reach following the drawn hands; a change of shape at every step, each
visible in silhouette from 10 blocks and creepier than the last; a little more danger at the top (from growth 3 the spit
throws two more seeds at the same gaps; at growth 5 the hop comes again after its own full tell); the stills, the shudder
take and the growth-5 fight again, the fight with the camera never inside the body, plus `grown_fight_phone.mp4` under
28 MB. Round 1's files went to `work/tomato/grown/raw/round1/`.

### What was built (round 2)

- `Growth.SIZE_STEP` 0.08. `TomatoDevilEntity.slapReach()`, `slapHalfWidth()`, `hopReach()` scale with its size (the
  slap's hands are drawn landing 3.5 model units ahead, so 4.9 blocks at growth 5); the moves and the fighter use them.
- `spitCount()`: 7 from growth 3 (`SPIT_MORE_AT`), at the same 26-degree gaps. The hop at growth 5 (`HOP_AGAIN_AT`) begins
  again from the top when it ends (a full squat as its tell, aimed anew), once.
- `client/devil/TomatoGrowthShape` draws the new shape from the Tomato's own meshes: 1, two eyes on stalks out of its top
  (and a long seam across the top); 2, an outer row of lips and teeth round the mouth (wider, taller, gaping) and a wet
  lobe swelling from its right side (the body mesh again, small, mouth turned in); 3, two small arms pushing out of its
  flanks, twitching and grasping; 4, those arms full size at its sides, stepping with their neighbours, two more stalk
  eyes, the lobe split open with eyes in it; 5, a second lobe on its left with its own mouth breathing open, five vine
  stems trailing over its sides, eight small eyes crowding in, six stalk eyes. The darker skin, seams, veins and drips
  stay. Stalk and crowding eyes have no red rim (it read as mushroom caps).
