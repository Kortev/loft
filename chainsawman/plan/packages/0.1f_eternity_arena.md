## Why it is boring today

It copies the show's staging: cut the room nonstop until a bar fills (4 min with saws, 8.5 min with a sword).
Any wall is a target, so there is nothing to read and nothing to dodge for.

## The new fight (Fable's design; numbers are first guesses in named constants)

Show rules used: it feels pain; it grows with fear; its heart is hidden; broken by pain it offers its core and
begs. The core is a lump shaped like an 8 with an eye and a mouth on either side and two mouths in the middle.

1. **Cutting walls does nothing.** Pain comes only from tearing off what attacks you, and from the core.
2. **Every attack is a limb that can be torn off.** Keep the four attacks (slam, head, squeeze, grab) with
   their tells. An attack that misses leaves its limb stuck for 50 ticks. Cut it then: two full sword hits, or
   one saw slash, tears it off for good (a stump stays, bleeding; the room visibly loses parts). A limb that hit
   you pulls back at once. A hit on a limb that isn't stuck does nothing but a flinch.
3. **Rounds.** Round 1: one attack at a time; tear off 4 limbs. Then it screams, the room convulses, and **the
   core is forced out** of the far wall on a stalk at chest height for 7 s, with a tell (the wall bulges and
   splits, 20 ticks). Only then can the core be hurt: a third of its health a window (about five sword hits, or
   two seconds of saws). If the window ends early, two more limbs bring it back. Round 2: 5 limbs, attacks come
   in pairs but a second tell never starts until the first has landed or missed; the long walls move in 2
   blocks. Round 3: 6 limbs, shorter rests, the walls move in again. After the third window it breaks: the
   ending that exists today (it begs, the core is held out) stays as it is.
4. **It grows with fear.** If nobody has torn a limb for 10 s, or a player is hit twice within 6 s, it laughs
   and one torn limb grows back (at most one per 10 s, never during a core window). Standing off costs progress.
5. **Never unfair.** One readable tell at a time per player; nothing hits from behind without a sound cue and
   a floor tell; after any hit the player has 20 ticks in which nothing new starts on them.
6. **Not only for Chainsaw Man.** The fight is the same with a sword. Saws tear limbs in one slash and empty a
   core window faster. Targets: saws about 90 s, iron sword alone about 2.5 min, two friends with swords about
   100 s. More players: more limbs at once (one tell per player), core health scaled.
7. Dying inside still throws you out of the hotel; the fight resets when nobody is left (as today).

## One look for the inside (no mix of blocks and model)

Inside the devil nothing drawn may be a plain block. The ten stills of what looks bad are in
`work/hotel/walk/looks_bad.jpg` (numbers 4-9 are the arena and the mouth).

- The arena's walls, floor and ceiling are **one flesh model** in the same style as the limbs, drawn over
  collision that is not itself drawn (an invisible, unbreakable block of our own, or the current blocks hidden:
  your call). No checkerboard, no glowstone squares, no flat coloured planes.
- **One pixel size everywhere:** every model texel is 1/16 of a block, like Minecraft's own pixels, shaded by
  its texture and flat lighting, not smooth blobs. Limbs, heads, teeth, walls, core: all the same.
- **Everything joins.** A limb or head comes out of the wall through a collar of flesh; teeth sit in gums;
  nothing floats or pokes through. Stumps of torn limbs stay as part of the wall.
- **One light.** The room is lit from itself (dim wet red, brighter veins), the same on walls, limbs and
  players; never dark (keep "every floor block lit 12+").
- **Tells live in the flesh:** the floor's veins light along the lane or arc an attack will take; no
  translucent sheets. Still readable at a glance.
- The walls moving in (rounds 2 and 3) move model and collision together.
- The mouth and the fall (stills 4 and 5): gums, a wet throat with depth, no flat orange plane.
- Creepy: the heads in the walls watch the player; the core's eyes look at whoever is cutting it and its mouths
  move; the laugh when it regrows; many voices at once (synthesised only).

## What was built

Branch `eternity-arena` (merged `claude/chainsaw-man` with 0.1g in the middle). Films in `work/hotel/arena2/`: `fight_sword.mp4`,
`fight_saws.mp4` (and `_phone.mp4` copies), `arena_sheet.jpg`, `arena_look.jpg`, raw captures under `raw\`.

The fight (`hotel/Shaft`, `ShaftArmEntity`, `ShaftHeadEntity`, `ShaftCoreEntity`, `EternityDevilEntity`):
1. Cutting walls does nothing (`cutBlock` swallows the blow, no pain, not counted as fighting; `cutAhead` cuts nothing); a blow on the
   sunk devil is nothing. Creeping flesh is gone from the arena (it was not a limb).
2. Slam, head, squeeze, grab keep their tells. A miss leaves the limb stuck `STUCK` = 50 ticks; `TEAR` = 12 tears it off (two full iron
   sword blows, one saw slash); a blow on a limb that isn't stuck is a flinch. A limb that hit pulls back at once; the grab holds 30 ticks
   (two sword blows free you, not torn). A squeeze that catches nobody leaves its thrust arm stuck out of the end wall. Torn limbs leave
   stumps (wall + place, synced to clients in the devil's tracked `arena` NBT).
3. Rounds: `NEED` = 6 / 7 / 8 limbs (+1 per extra player), then a scream, the far end wall bulges 20 ticks and the core comes out on a
   stalk, chest high, for 140 ticks; `WINDOW_HP` = 30 a window (x1.6 per extra player). Let go by: drawn back, two more limbs bring it back.
   Rounds 2 and 3: pairs (the second 6 ticks after the first resolves), the long walls move in a block each (model and `ArenaFleshBlock`
   collision together, players pushed out, put back on mend). Third window emptied: `arenaTo(1)`, and the ending is the old one (begs, core
   held out in the middle, refuse, cut it).
4. Grows with fear: 10 s with no limb torn, or a player hit twice within 6 s: it laughs ("More. Be more afraid.", a synthesised crowd laugh)
   and one torn limb of this round grows back (at most one per 10 s, never in a window). The bar goes back with it.
5. One tell per player; nothing new on a player for 20 ticks after a hit; nothing new while a limb lies stuck and nothing else is out
   (`STUCK_REST` 26 ticks), so a pair never buries a stuck limb (added after the first sword take stalled in round 3 with 8 regrows).
6. Numbers changed from the brief's guesses: NEED 4/5/6 -> 6/7/8 and REST 22-ish -> 40/34/26, to bring the fights toward the targets.

The look: `tools/eternity_3d_room.py` bakes `eternity_room` (floor, ceiling, throat with rings, gums and teeth, the mouth's hollow, four
walls each its own part, collars, stumps, limb and neck pieces, the big hand), every part at 1/16 block drawn at scale 1; floor veins are
exported in its json and lit for tells (`ArenaRenderer`). The shaft mass is now 1/16 and fills the hollow, its hole open into the throat
(no pit floor, no orange plane); the core is rebaked at 1/16. All arena blocks are `ArenaFleshBlock` (no outline, full collision, opaque,
drawn by nothing). Old translucent lanes, sheets, light columns, squeeze bulges and wall veins are gone. Heads in the walls are set in
rings of flesh and watch the nearest player; the core's face turns on whoever cut it last.

Coordinates: the arena, `Arena`, `EternityDevilEntity`, `ArenaRenderer` and `HotelFight` use `HotelBuilder.at`/`localOf`/`dir`; the old
point frame (`world(double...)`, `local`) is removed; tests check the arena at turns 1-3.

4 hits taken, 1 limb grew back (an earlier take on the same numbers: 100.7 s, 3 hits). Saws: landing to beg 77.7 s, fall to core cut
84 s, 15 hits taken (the form fighter stands in closer). Before the
stuck-rest rule the sword took 193 s with 8 regrows. The scripted fighter is near perfect; a real player will be slower.
