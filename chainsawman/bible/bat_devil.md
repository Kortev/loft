# Bat Devil
Checked on 2026-11-11. Covers up to chapter 52 only. Wiki read through its API (wikitext).

## Looks
- An enormous muscular humanoid bat; grotesque face, many sharp teeth, big round eyes. (one source: wiki, ch 6-7)
- Arms join the torso by skin flaps that are also his wings. (one source: wiki, ch 7)
- Colour: dark purple in the manga; plain dark grey in the anime. (one source: wiki)
- Size: far bigger than a person; holds Denji in one hand and swallows Power and a birdcage whole. (wiki + search, ch 6-7)
- He wears a birdcage on a chain round his neck, holding Meowy the cat. (one source: wiki, ch 7)
- Right arm is missing when first seen; it regrows after he drinks Denji's blood. (wiki + search, ch 6)

## Does
- Ch 6 / ep 3: waits in a farmhouse, takes Meowy hostage so Power will bring him a human to drink. (wiki + search)
- Squeezes Denji in one fist and drinks the blood that runs out; the arm regrows. Calls it disgusting. (one source: wiki, ch 6)
- Punches up through the roof of his hiding place in one blow. (one source: wiki, ch 6)
- Swallows the cage with Meowy, then Power, in single gulps, as punishment. (wiki + search, ch 7)
- Spreads his wings and takes off over the roof toward the city, flying fast. (one source: wiki, ch 7)
- Denji bites his leg mid-air; he squeezes Denji to death; Denji pulls the cord and cuts him; they fall through a roof. (one source: wiki, ch 7-8)
- In the street he throws a car one-handed, expecting Denji to save the person inside; Denji throws it back. (one source: wiki, ch 8)
- Mouth cannon: his mouth stretches into a gun barrel and fires a huge air blast that smashes buildings and throws Denji into one. (one source: wiki, ch 8)
- Last: grabs a human to drink and heal, then throws a punch; Denji sticks a chainsaw in the arm and rides it up to the head. (one source: wiki, ch 8)

## Rules
- Wild devil; hates humans, loves eating them, especially children and healthy people. (one source: wiki, ch 7)
- Drinking blood heals him and regrows limbs. (one source: wiki, ch 1, 6)
- Tough: survives wall-smashes, a long fall and a car in the face with minor cuts. (one source: wiki)
- Smells humans from a far building. (one source: wiki, ch 7)
- Beaten by Denji cutting him to pieces from the arm up; denied blood. (one source: wiki, ch 8)
- Drawn from the fear of bats. (one source: wiki trivia)
- Has a girlfriend, the Leech Devil, who mourns him. (one source: wiki, ch 9)

## Said
- Complains Denji and Power taste disgusting. (one source: wiki, ch 6-7)
- Shocked that a human-looking Denji is a devil; puzzled that a devil lets a human escape. (one source: wiki, ch 8)
- Voice: deep, male, gloating. (guess; wiki credits only voice actors)

## Not known
- Exact height or wingspan.
- How often the mouth cannon can fire; seen once.
- Whether he has any contract; none shown.
