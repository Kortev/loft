# Leech Devil
Checked on 2026-11-09. Covers up to chapter 52 only.

## Looks
- A big wrinkled body on four legs, plus two tentacle-like arms. (one source: wiki, ch 9)
- Every limb ends in a round sucker mouth like a leech's. (one source: wiki)
- Her face is just one large mouth full of blocky teeth. (one source: wiki)
- Long black hair, and three pairs of udder-like glands on the body. (one source: wiki)
- Colour: yellowish in the coloured manga; dark, real-leech colours in the anime. (one source: wiki)
- Size: much bigger than Denji; the Fox's head takes her whole head in one bite. (wiki + search)
- **Scale, from kortev who has seen it (2026-11-11): in the anime this is an epic fight against a giant, not a
  crawling thing a few blocks tall.** The wiki gives no measurement. Ours: about 9 blocks tall, 16 long [pick].

## Does
- Ch 9 / ep 4: just after Denji kills the Bat Devil and frees Power and Meowy, she arrives and blasts a hole through his forearm, taking his hand off. (wiki + search)
- She picks up the hand and eats it. (wiki + anime ep 4 transcript)
- Denji pulls his cord but is too low on blood; the chainsaw only half comes out of his head. (one source: wiki)
- She offers to let him go because he's cute, but means to kill Power and Meowy; he stays to protect them. (wiki + anime ep 4 transcript)
- They trade blows; Denji ties her leg with the Bat Devil's guts to trip her. (one source: search recap)
- Denji cuts her once, but she runs him through the stomach with her tongue. (one source: wiki, ch 10)
- As she moves to eat him, Aki calls the Fox, which bites her head off and swallows it. (wiki + search)

## Rules
- She is a wild devil, not contracted to anyone. (one source: wiki)
- She was the Bat Devil's girlfriend and came for revenge. (wiki + search)
- Her tongue is strong enough to pierce a body with ease. (one source: wiki)
- Power from the fear of leeches. (one source: wiki trivia)
- How she is beaten in the show: not by Denji; the Fox kills her in one bite. (wiki + search)

## Said
- Calls the Bat Devil fondly by a pet name and mourns him. (wiki + anime ep 4 transcript)
- Says the two of them dreamed of eating all humans together, an impossible but beautiful dream. (wiki + anime ep 4 transcript)
- Talks down to Denji as a little puppy, teasing and sing-song, then turns murderous. (wiki + anime ep 4 transcript)
- Scorns Denji's dream as trashy; he calls their fight a battle of dreams. (wiki + search)
- Female voice in Japanese; male voice actor credited in the English dub. (one source: wiki infobox)

## Not known
- Exact height or length; no measurement given.
- How fast she moves or whether she climbs; she is only seen in one street fight.
- What the "blast" through Denji's arm is (tongue, sucker or something else); the wiki does not say.
