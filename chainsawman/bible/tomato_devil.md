# Tomato Devil
Checked on 2026-11-09. Covers up to chapter 52 only.

## Looks
- A big round red body shaped like a tomato. (wiki + search)
- Many bulging eyes sprout from the body in every direction. (wiki + search)
- A large vertical mouth down the middle of the body. (wiki + search)
- It stands on eight human-like arms growing from its underside. (one source: wiki, ch 1)
- Size: big enough for Denji to stand on its corpse afterwards. (one source: search recap)

## Does
- It is the first devil in the story (ch 1 / ep 1): Pochita barks to say a devil is near, and Denji finds it. (wiki + search)
- Where: the manga summary has it walking around near a house; the anime episode doesn't place it clearly. (one source: wiki)
- Denji, still human, uses Pochita as a hand-held chainsaw and cuts it up. (wiki + search)
- The yakuza debt collector who hired him takes the carcass. (wiki + anime ep 1 transcript)

## Rules
- It comes back to life from its seeds, so the seeds must be burned; Denji tells his boss this. (wiki + anime ep 1 transcript)
- It is weak, because people don't fear tomatoes much. (wiki + search)
- Its body sells on the black market: the collector pays 400,000 yen for it. (wiki + anime ep 1 transcript)
- Debt, interest and fees take that down to 70,000 yen; after bills Denji has almost nothing. (wiki + anime ep 1 transcript)
- Denji says a dead devil usually fetches about 300,000 yen, so this one paid above normal. (wiki + anime ep 1 transcript)
- A wild devil; power from the fear of tomatoes. (one source: wiki)

## Said
- It never speaks in the show. (one source: wiki; no lines in the ep 1 transcript)

## Not known
- Exact size in metres; not given.
- How it fights; it is killed before it does anything on screen. (search: abilities not explained)
- What the seeds look like or how they regrow; only the rule is told.
