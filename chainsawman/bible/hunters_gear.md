# Public Safety hunters: gear, office, pay
Checked on 2026-11-09. Covers up to chapter 52 only.

## Looks
- Public Safety has a dress code: formal suit and tie; private hunters have none. (wiki + search)
- The usual look is white shirt, black tie, black trousers; some wear the suit jacket, some don't (Denji wears his loosely, sleeves rolled). (wiki + search)
- The issued suits are reinforced and give some protection. (one source: wiki)
- Hunters carry badges for ID. (one source: wiki)
- Makima hands Denji his uniform on arrival at headquarters, saying they're meant to wear it. (wiki + anime ep 2 transcript)
- Aki carries his sword on his back. (one source: wiki)
- Hunters and police are the only people legally allowed guns. (one source: wiki)
- Hunters are the only people the government allows to make devil contracts. (one source: wiki)

## Does
- Patrols and small jobs go out in two-person teams, as a precaution. (one source: anime ep 2 transcript)
- They're called out like a fire brigade or police when a devil threatens the public. (one source: wiki)
- They must write detailed reports on what they do. (one source: wiki)
- Killing a devil a private hunter already reported counts as obstructing justice (Power does this with the Sea Cucumber Devil). (wiki + anime ep 2 transcript)
- The office as a place: a Tokyo headquarters building, and Makima's office where she calls them in. (wiki + search)

## Rules
- Pay: Public Safety hunters get a regular government salary; private hunters live on bounties. (one source: wiki)
- Makima sells it as the best benefits and lots of paid leave. (wiki + anime ep 2 transcript)
- A dead devil is usually worth about 300,000 yen. (wiki + anime ep 1 transcript)
- Denji's salary goes into Aki's bank account and he gets 10,000 yen a month. (one source: wiki, citing a guidebook)
- Many hunters join for the paycheck, others for revenge on the Gun Devil. (one source: wiki)
- Over a thousand hunters in Tokyo, counting private ones. (wiki + anime ep 2 transcript)
- Rank: spoken of as seniority, not titles; Aki "started three years before" Denji, Himeno is Aki's senior. (one source each: ep 2 transcript; wiki ch 10)
- Partners: Makima assigns Denji to Aki; Aki is Himeno's sixth partner and the five before him died. (wiki + anime ep 2 and ep 5 transcripts)
- Special Division 4 is an experimental unit; the higher-ups will break it up if it gets no results. (wiki + anime ep 2 transcript)
- Other divisions exist (Division 2 is seen in ch 46). (one source: wiki)
- Hunters shouldn't reveal their devil contract in public. (one source: anime ep 7 transcript)

## Said
- Public Safety is described as dangerous but popular; even schools have devil-hunting clubs. (one source: wiki)

## Not known
- What the rooms look like (desks, corridors, briefing room); no source describes the interior.
- Any named rank system ("first class" etc.); only weak fan pages use such labels, so treat as unconfirmed.
- Standard-issue weapons; only personal ones (Aki's sword) are described.
