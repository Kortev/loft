# Eternity Devil
Checked on 2026-11-11 by Fable, on the wiki page read in the browser. Covers the hotel arc only (chapters 14-19,
anime episodes 5-7). The page also has later material: none of it is here, and none of it may be used.

## Looks
- Its **core** is a lump of flesh shaped like the number 8 (or the infinity sign), with an eye and a mouth on
  either side and two mouths in the centre. (wiki, ch 19 p 18)
- Inside its own stomach it shows as an endless wave of flesh: many arms and heads fused together. (wiki, ch 14)
- It starts very small and weak (one head on the floor) and grows fast. (wiki, ch 14)

## Does
- Turns one floor of a building (the hotel's eighth) into a loop: every way out leads back, all rooms link to
  each other. That floor is its stomach. (wiki, ch 15)
- Time is stopped inside: every clock reads 8:18 and the second hand snaps back. (wiki, ch 15 p 19)
- Its body grows until it blocks a room and part of the corridor, then chases people down the corridor. (wiki,
  ch 16-17)
- It tilts the hotel's floor. (wiki, ch 17)
- It fights with its many arms and heads, strong enough to tear a transformed Chainsaw Man apart. (wiki, ch 19)

## Rules
- **It grows with the fear of the people trapped in it.** Panic makes it bigger. (wiki, ch 17)
- **Its heart is not on the eighth floor.** It says so: they are in its stomach and cannot kill it there. (wiki,
  ch 17 p 3-5)
- **It feels pain**, and it is afraid of the chainsaws: it tries to kill him indirectly. (wiki, ch 18 p 9)
- Blood from its wounds refuels Chainsaw Man. (wiki, ch 19)
- **How it is beaten:** about three days of nonstop cutting. It breaks from the pain, **offers up its core and
  begs for death**; the core is cut in two. (wiki, ch 19 p 17-19)
- It makes deals to get what it wants: it offers to let the others go if they give it one of them. (wiki, ch 16)
- The Fox cannot be called onto it from inside. (wiki, ch 17)

## Said
- It laughs at people's panic. It boasts that their deaths are certain and tells them to be more afraid. (wiki)
- Many voices at once (the page credits a crowd of voice actors).

## Not known
- Where the heart is, if not on that floor. What the core looks like in colour.

## For the mod (Fable's reading)
- The show gives the core only at the very end, offered by the devil. Showing it earlier, in flashes when the
  pain peaks, is ours.
- "Grows with fear" is a rule we can play: it gets bigger while players run or take hits, smaller when cut.
