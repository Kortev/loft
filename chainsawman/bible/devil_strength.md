# Devil strength (chapters 1-52 / season 1 / Reze arc)

Summary: in the early story a devil's power is tied to how feared its name is; blood heals devils and hybrids; nothing early says a single devil grows with age or by feeding.

Source note: the Fandom wiki pages blocked automated fetching (HTTP 402), so evidence comes from web-search excerpts of wiki and recap pages. Scene references are approximate; verify before relying on exact chapter numbers.

## The five claims

1. Strength comes from how feared the name is. Verdict: STATED.
Makima tells Denji that all devils are born with names and the more those names are feared, the more powerful the devil is said to become (early season 1 / early chapters). The Gun Devil's power is framed as coming from fear of guns.
Sources: https://comicbook.com/anime/news/chainsaw-man-devils-explained-powers-transformations-anime ; https://www.sportskeeda.com/anime/chainsaw-man-anime-devils-devil-hunters-explained

2. Stronger the longer it has been alive. Verdict: NOT SUPPORTED.
No early scene or line ties a devil's power to its age. Strength is described by name and fear only.
Source: same as claim 1 (no age rule appears in any result).

3. Stronger or healing by drinking blood / eating. Verdict: SHOWN (healing only).
The Bat Devil regrows a severed arm after drinking Denji's blood and complains of the taste, saying it needs to eat humans to get rid of it (Bat Devil fight, season 1 ep. 4-5). Denji heals by drinking the Eternity Devil's blood (season 1 ep. 7). This is recovery, not shown as lasting extra strength.
Sources: https://wegotthiscovered.com/?p=1331551 ; https://www.sportskeeda.com/anime/chainsaw-man-episode-7-the-eternity-devil-s-defeat-grants-denji-shocking-first-kiss

4. Weaker when people stop fearing or forget its name. Verdict: NOT ESTABLISHED BY THE REZE ARC.
Not established by the Reze arc.

5. Anything else that grows one individual devil over time. Verdict: NOT SUPPORTED.
Nothing in the early story shows an individual devil leveling up. Differences in power come from which name the devil has (Tomato and Fish are concrete names; Future and Curse are abstract, per wiki note) and from fiend/contract arrangements with humans, not from personal growth.
Source: https://chainsaw-man.fandom.com/wiki/Power?oldid=7483 (cited via search excerpt only)

## Guesses
- Fear-scaled power with no growth rule suggests a devil's strength is fixed by its name when it spawns; any growth in the mod is our own invention.
- Blood as healing fits the Bat Devil and Denji; using it as a heal-on-hit mechanic matches the show, using it as permanent growth does not.
