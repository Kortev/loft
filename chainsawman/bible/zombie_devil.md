# Zombie Devil
Checked on 2026-11-11. Covers up to chapter 52 only. Wiki read through its API (wikitext).

## Looks
- A giant floating torso with no limbs and several exposed organs, including a brain. (one source: wiki, ch 1)
- Tentacles run from the brain to the zombies in his horde. (one source: wiki, ch 1)
- A large human-like face sits on the right side of the torso, away from where a head would be. (one source: wiki, ch 1)
- Colour in the anime: not stated. Size: bigger than a person; no measurement. (one source: wiki)
- He loses his right eye and is cut in two in the fight. (one source: wiki, ch 1)

## Does
- Ch 1 / ep 1: calls Denji to an abandoned building; the old man and men are already zombies. (wiki + search)
- Orders the zombies to kill Denji; they throw the body in a dumpster. (wiki + search)
- When Denji walks out alive, he is surprised and sends the zombies to eat him. (one source: wiki, ch 1)
- Denji pulls the cord, shreds the zombies, then jams a chainsaw into the devil's head. (wiki + search)
- Denji then kills every remaining zombie. (one source: wiki, ch 1)
- He floats, so he attacks only through the horde. (guess)

## Zombies
- Made by the devil's power; they were people who made a deal with him. (one source: wiki, ch 1)
- A bite turns a person into a zombie. (one source: wiki, ch 32)
- They obey him as loyal servants. (one source: wiki, ch 1)
- They keep moving and attacking when badly hurt, so they are tougher than people. (one source: wiki)
- Left over after he was beaten, a cluster remained and was held as a gun-agent weapon. (one source: wiki, ch 32)
- Shown only as a horde in a closed room; walk speed is not stated. (guess)

## Rules
- Wild devil; made a contract offering power to the yakuza at an unknown price; it made them his slaves. (one source: wiki, ch 1)
- Hates humans, most of all devil hunters, who killed his kind. (one source: wiki, ch 1)
- Cunning: tricked the yakuza. (one source: wiki)
- Blood should heal him (guess from the general devil rule; wiki says presumably).
- Beaten by a chainsaw to the head. (wiki + search, ch 1)
- Drawn from the fear of the living dead. (one source: wiki trivia)

## Said
- Gloats that the yakuza became his willing slaves, and that his power makes zombies. (one source: wiki quote, ch 1)
- Voice: male in Japanese, taunting. (one source: wiki infobox; tone is a guess)

## Not known
- What the zombies do when he dies; ch 1 shows Denji killing them too, so no answer.
- How many zombies he can control at once.
- The exact price in the contract.
