# Fox Devil
Checked on 2026-11-09. Covers up to chapter 52 only.

## Looks
- A huge arctic-fox shape; what comes through is usually just the giant head. (wiki + search)
- The head is covered in many big eyes, each drawn as rings inside rings. (one source: wiki, ch 10)
- Colours: white fur with glowing red-orange eyes in the anime; orange or light-brown fur in the coloured manga. (one source: wiki)
- Other hunters can call a huge clawed foreleg instead, also covered in eyes. (one source: wiki, ch 46)
- Size: the head alone is big enough to bite a devil's head off and swallow a grown man whole. (wiki + search)

## Does
- Ch 10 / ep 4: the Leech Devil has Denji pinned and is about to eat him; Aki arrives, makes the hand sign, says the word. (wiki + search)
- The head appears above the Leech Devil and bites its head off, then politely asks Aki if it may swallow; he says yes. (wiki + search)
- It swallows, then vanishes in a puff of smoke. (wiki + search)
- Ch 23 / ep 8: ambushed by an attacker, Aki has it swallow him whole; it grumbles that the thing is neither human nor devil, and he cuts his way out through its head. (wiki + search)
- It heals fully after its head is slashed open. (one source: wiki)
- Ch 46: called by a Division 2 hunter to swallow an attacker, it complains of the taste and vanishes at once, leaving her. (wiki + search; the taste complaint is one source)

## Rules
- Call: hand shaped like a fox's head, and the word "Kon", the Japanese sound a fox makes. (wiki + search)
- Price: the contractor feeds it bits of their own body; Aki says this time it was some skin. (wiki + anime ep 4 transcript)
- Its real body lives in Kyoto; it travels to its contractors when called. (wiki + search)
- Who may contract: it is friendly to humans and has contracts with many hunters (Aki, Arai and others). (wiki + anime ep 7 transcript)
- Only good-looking contractors get to call the head. (wiki + anime ep 7 transcript)
- Ch 17 / ep 6: inside the Eternity Devil's sealed, looped floor it does not come; Aki says they are cut off from the world, Himeno says it makes sense since its body is in Kyoto. (wiki + anime ep 6 transcript)
- Ch 30: it refuses to come for Aki in hospital; Kurose guesses Aki used it too recklessly and it has soured on him for good. (wiki + search)
- It has no loyalty to contractors and leaves when it dislikes the taste of what it is fed. (one source: wiki)
- Nothing found saying it refuses darkness, night, caves or any time of day. The only "won't come" cases: a sealed-off space (too far from Kyoto) and a contractor it has fallen out with. (wiki + search)

## Said
- Cheerful about eating: asks permission before swallowing a devil. (wiki + search)
- Complains, with disgust, when made to eat something that is not a plain human or devil. (wiki + search)
- Female voice in the anime; Aki and Himeno call it "she". (one source: anime transcripts)

## Not known
- Exact size in blocks or metres; the show never measures it.
- How much body counts as one payment beyond "some skin", or whether the price grows with use.
- What a contractor who is not good-looking calls instead (only the foreleg is seen).
