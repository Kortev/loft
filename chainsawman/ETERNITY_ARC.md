# The Eternity Devil arc, and what it means for the mod (2026-11-10)

Sources: the Chainsaw Man wiki (https://chainsaw-man.fandom.com/wiki/Eternity_Devil, read 2026-11-10; kortev: base
everything on the wiki) and episode recaps (anime season 1, episodes 5-7; manga chapters 14-19). Spoiler-safe: the
wiki page also covers far later chapters; nothing from them is used or written here.

## From the wiki (the source of truth)

- **Its core (what it offers up when it begs):** a lump of flesh in the shape of the number 8, which reads as the
  infinity sign. It has an eye and a mouth on either side and two mouths in the centre (manga chapter 19, page 18).
  When it breaks, it offers up this core, begs for death, and Denji cuts it in two.
- **Its avatar (what fights):**
  - inside its own stomach, an **endless wave of flesh made of many arms and heads fused together**;
  - it starts very small and weak (first a disembodied head, which Power slices in two) and grows exponentially,
    until it blocks off a room and a section of corridor;
  - its many arms and heads tear Denji apart even transformed, and once snap his neck.
- **Fear feeds it:** "I expand with fear, and ensnare in fear" (its own words, paraphrased). Kobeni's and Arai's panic
  makes it grow.
- **The pocket reality:**
  - it turns a whole floor into a looped piece of 3D space, its stomach, with all the rooms linked to each other;
  - time is stopped: every clock shows **8:18**, and the second hand keeps snapping back;
  - the looped rooms repeat with identical contents.
- **In the arc:**
  - it offers a contract: it frees them if they feed it Denji;
  - Aki can't summon the Fox over it, and Himeno's ghost only enrages it;
  - it says its heart isn't on the 8th floor and they're in its stomach, so it can't be killed;
  - it tilts the hotel floor and demands Denji's heart (episode 6: "The floor then turns 90 degrees"; the corridor
    becomes a vertical shaft with the devil filling its bottom, and its mouth is a pit in a floor of fused arms and
    heads that Denji drops into from a room's doorway; the manga panel shows this from above);
  - Denji notices it's afraid of his chainsaws and jumps into its mouth, pulling his cord;
  - about three days of nonstop fighting, Denji drinking its blood, Himeno pulling his cord once to bring him back;
  - then it breaks.
- **Trivia:**
  - it draws its power from apeirophobia, the fear of infinity;
  - the 8s (the 8th floor, 8:18) and the figure-8 core are the infinity sign.

**What this changes for the design:** our devil (lobes with eyes and teeth pouring into one maw with an anatomical
heart) is not the wiki's.
- The fighting body should be **a wave of fused human arms and heads**.
- The heart should be **the figure-8 core** with its two eyes and four mouths.
- It should **start as one small head and grow** with fear.

The arc as the recaps tell it:

## What happens

- **The job:** Public Safety sends Special Division 4 (Denji, Power, Aki, Himeno, Kobeni, Arai) to a hotel where
  people have vanished. A devil there has eaten a piece of the Gun Devil, which makes it far stronger. Himeno offers
  a kiss to whoever kills it, which gets Denji going.
- **The trap:**
  - They're stuck on the **8th floor**: the stairs up or down come back to the 8th floor, the lift loops, and windows
    only look into the next room.
  - **Every clock is stopped at 8:18.** Time is frozen on the floor, so no help will ever come. The power still works.
  - They're stuck for days and track time by sleep and hunger. There's little food (Power eats it), and starvation
    is the threat.
  - The floor is the devil's stomach.
- **The devil shows itself:**
  - A weak devil Power had already killed re-forms in the hallway, balloons into a giant mass and names itself the
    Eternity Devil.
  - Its core is a knot of flesh bent into a **figure 8 (the infinity sign)**, with an eye and a mouth on each loop
    and a pair of mouths between them. Inside it's an endless churning mass of fused arms, heads, eyes and limbs.
  - It swells to fill the hallway. When their fear spikes, it **tilts the whole floor**, so anyone leaving a room
    falls down the corridor toward its mouths.
  - It's cruel, laughs at fear and feeds on it. It suppresses powers inside: Aki can't summon the Fox, and Himeno's
    ghost only angers it.
- **The deal:**
  - It offers to free everyone if they give it Denji, even dead. It's bound by the contract: break it and it dies.
  - Arai and Kobeni want to do it (Kobeni stabs at Denji and hits Aki). Aki refuses. Himeno is torn, but won't let
    Aki pay years of his life for his sword.
- **The fight:**
  - Denji gives himself up on purpose: he jumps into its mouth, transforms mid-jump, bursts out through its teeth and
    shreds it from inside.
  - Low on blood, his chainsaws retract, until he eats its flesh and realises that **drinking its blood lets him
    fight forever**.
  - **Its heart isn't on that floor, so the body can't be killed.** Denji wins by endurance: about three days of
    nonstop pain, until the devil **presents its own heart and begs to die**. He cuts the heart in half.
  - They walk out drenched in blood. Denji collapses from blood loss, happy.
- **Tone:** the despair of a frightened group trapped forever, sacrificing a friend against sacrificing yourself, and
  Denji cheerful through it. The comedy: Power enjoying Kobeni's tears, Denji napping.

| The floor is its stomach; time frozen; no help | sealed hotel, no help | Matches |

Core (its heart): a figure-8 lump, an eye and a mouth per loop, two mouths in the centre | a mass of lobes pouring into one great maw, eyes everywhere, an anatomical heart | **The big gap** (wiki): redesign the body as fused arms and heads, and the heart as the figure-8 core |
