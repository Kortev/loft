# Gojolander

A Fabric mod for Minecraft Java **1.21.1**: the first friend's own mod on the SMP, beside kortev's
[The Shooting Star](../README.md) and in the same repository and build. He plays the strongest there is, and kortev's
rival. It needs The Shooting Star installed: it is under that mod's rule that only a thing's owner crafts it (crafters do
not make it at all), and only for **him**. Here, his things work for him alone too: on anyone else the blindfold is a
strip of black cloth. (Until he is made its owner, it is open to everyone: see below.)

For now he has one thing, the blindfold: what he sees through it, and what he casts with it: his Domain, and Hollow Purple.

## The blindfold

A band of black cloth tied over the eyes, knotted at the back with its two ends hanging. It is worn where a helmet would
be, and keeps out nothing a helmet would: wearing it is going without one.

- **Getting it:** echo shard over black wool, a nether star and black wool, over an echo shard. Only its owner can
  craft it.
- **Whose it is:** nobody's until kortev has his Minecraft name, and **while it is nobody's, anyone can craft it and
  cast with it**, to try it out. An operator makes it his alone on the server with `/gojolander owner <name>` (no new
  jar needed); the world keeps it, and from then on only he crafts it and only he can cast. (The stand-in for nobody,
  `-unclaimed-` in `Owner.PLACEHOLDER`, is a name no account can have.)

## The Six Eyes

The blindfold does not blind him; it is how he sees. With it on, every living thing within **24 blocks**
(`sixEyesRange`) shows to him through whatever stands between them: walls, hills, the dark, outlined in the cold blue
of his eyes. Only he sees it, and only with the blindfold on; at 0 the rule turns it off.

## Domain Expansion: Unlimited Void

His ult. With the blindfold on, he presses **Z** (rebindable: Domain Expansion). Second by second:

- **0 to 1.5 s, the eyes.** A hard cut to his face, close, the camera drifting slowly round him as his left hand comes
  up to his brow, hooks the blindfold and pushes it up onto his forehead, his head dipping a little, and his eyes open,
  burning blue; the hand drops. A breath drawn in; a cold chime as they light. Round him a ring of ink starts out over
  the ground, ragged, fingers of it branching ahead. The blindfold's ends swing, and whip about as the black closes.
- **1.5 to 1.9 s, the sign.** His right hand snaps up in front of his chin, the first two fingers crossed, and the
  camera punches in on it. DOMAIN EXPANSION comes up on the screen, and he says it, in his own voice from the show:
  "Ryōiki Tenkai" (heard by everyone in reach, from where he stands).
- **1.9 to 4.3 s, the barrier.** A cut to wide, the camera craning back and up round him. The ink reaches its full
  32 blocks at 2.5 s and the black floods up out of it like a liquid, fingers racing ahead of a white-hot curling lip,
  drips running back down, the air drawn in from every side faster and faster. **Anyone who gets out of the ring
  before it closes is not caught**: about 4 s from the key, against a sprint of a block every 3.6 ticks.
- **4.3 s, it closes.** One deep, soft blow and a glassy ring, cut off at once. Every living thing inside it but him
  is **caught**: players and mobs, in the air or not (not players in creative or spectator, and not bosses). For
  everyone in it, black and silence; his camera is back in his eyes. From outside it is a sphere of black, its skin
  rippling, stars deep inside it, the air bending round its edge and ink pooled at its foot.
- **5 s, the void opens** out of the great eye, over the whole sky, with one deep, slow, struck boom ringing cold and far voices with no words, and him: "Muryōkūsho" (all round, for everyone in it). UNLIMITED VOID, and
  its name brushed in ink under it: 無量空処. Endless space, light flowing past in slow rivers that curve round the eye
  as they run in to it, the eye a black disc bending the stars round it in a ring of white fire, a band of light across
  it, and still black water underfoot giving it all back, rippling out round him. Whoever is in there with him is dark
  but for the eye's light along their edges. He is free to move, hit and use anything.
  - **The caught** are held exactly where they were for the whole of it: they cannot move, fall, look round, hit, use or
    break anything, and mobs stop thinking. Nothing knocks them about. Seen by anyone else they tremble where they
    hang, players with their heads tipped back, arms out from them. What they see is everything there is, all at
    once: the picture torn apart by light rushing in, flickering grids of meaning, flashes of colour and white; what
    they hear is a roar of data and voices and a ringing in the ears.
  - **It takes them down as it goes** (as in the show, where how long you are in it is how bad it is: a fifth of a
    second, and a crowd needs two months to come back). About every 2 s the flood surges: a dull blow inside their
    heads, the picture whiting out, and a step of the way down to half a heart, through any armour, enchantment,
    Resistance or shield. Everyone sees it: they flinch red, heads thrown back and arms flung out, with a muffled
    throb. It is not a wound: no cry of pain, no knock, nothing to bleed.
  - **Nothing crosses the barrier** either way while it stands. Whoever tries is put back where they were; anything
    fired at it or out of it stops against it, in a puff of ink.
- **15 to 16.5 s, it fades.** As in the show, when it has done its work it simply goes: the void and the barrier thin
  away like smoke, patch by patch, wisps of light along their going edges, the world showing through, with a long
  falling breath of sound. His left hand comes up again and pulls the blindfold back down over his eyes. The caught go
  free **broken: half a heart** (its last step), and slow, weak and half blind for 15 s. The ringing in their heads
  climbs and snaps off, and their knees give out: down onto them, head hung, for a little over a second (they can do
  nothing), then up again, dazed, faint pale motes of the void's light drifting off their heads. Their own view goes
  down with them, and through the daze they hear their own heart, slow and muffled, fading as it wears off. Anything
  with arms and legs like a player's (zombies, skeletons, piglins) goes down onto its knees too; other creatures sag.
  Whoever it held in the air goes down as they land. The finishing is left to him: his next blow lands as it would.
- **Holding on to him** spares you, as in the show ("as long as I'm touching you, you'll be fine"): a player crouched
  right against him as it closes is not caught. They see the void, but are not held, flooded or broken.
- **Cut short, it shatters** instead: cracks run out across it from the eye (from outside, from the side facing you),
  in long rays and rings, then the shards fall away nearest first, edges catching the light, the world behind and its
  air rushing back.

He can cast it again **15 minutes** after he cast it (the blindfold shows it cooling, through leaving and coming back
too). There is no other cost. It ends at once, shattering, if he dies or leaves (then the caught are only shaken, not
broken: they keep what its steps had taken by then, no more), or if kortev's Genesis Key takes the world; and it is not cast at all while a Genesis Key event is going on
(from the key turning until everyone is home). Killing him while he casts it, before it closes, stops it.

### Commands (operators)

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/gojolander owner [<name>]` | Shows, or sets, whose the blindfold is (nobody's, and so anyone's, until set). |
| `/gojolander domain <pos>` | Casts a Domain at `pos` with nobody casting it: everyone inside is caught. |
| `/gojolander collapse` | Shatters every Domain now. |
| `/gojolander ready <player>` | Makes that player's Domain ready again. |
| `/gojolander cast <player>` | As if that player had pressed the key (for trying it, or from a datapack): the same rules hold. |

### Game rules (for balancing it against everyone's mods)

| Rule | Default | Range | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| `unlimitedVoidRadius` | 32 | 8 to 64 | How far it reaches from where he casts it, in blocks. |
| `unlimitedVoidHoldSeconds` | 10 | 1 to 30 | How long the void stays open, holding the caught. |
| `unlimitedVoidCooldownSeconds` | 900 | 0 to 7200 | How long before he can cast it again. |
| `unlimitedVoidBreaks` | true | | Whether it takes whoever it holds down, step by step, to half a heart at its end. |
| `sixEyesRange` | 24 | 0 to 64 | How far he sees through things with the blindfold on (0: not at all). |

### With Sodium, Iris and shader packs

It works with Sodium and with Iris, shader packs on or off (tried with Complementary Reimagined). A shader pack draws
the world into its own pictures and lays them over the screen only at the very end, so with one in use the Domain is
drawn after the pack has finished each frame (shootingstar's `WorldPasses`, which kortev's own effects use too) instead
of at the end of the world's drawing.

## Hollow Purple

His second power, and the one people replay. With the blindfold on (his, by the same owner rule), he presses **N**
(rebindable: Hollow Purple; not X, which is Chitty's eject key and vanilla's Load Hotbar key: only one binding on a key hears it). About 4 s of cast, then the world is erased in a line.

- **0 to 1.2 s, Blue.** The camera cuts low in front of him to his left hand: his arm out, a blue orb forms at his
  fingertips, crackling, pulling dust, sparks and loose blocks into it, rings of light drawing in round it, the
  picture bending towards it, its light on the ground and on him.
- **1.2 to 2.4 s, Red.** A hard cut to the other side: his right arm out, a red orb throwing shock rings out, sparks and
  heat thrown off it, the picture bending away from it.
- **2.4 to 2.8 s, the sweep.** Front and centre, close: his hands sweep together across in front of him.
- **2.8 s, the merge.** The orbs meet: a white flash over the whole screen, a crack and a deep boom, a shell of light
  thrown out, the picture rumbling. **A huge violet sphere** (as big as its radius, 6 blocks across by default) with a
  hard white core swells out of it and is pushed ahead of his hands, its skin churning, arcs crawling over it, its violet
  light on everything near. HOLLOW PURPLE comes up on the screen, and 虚式「茈」 brushed under it.
- **3.8 s, the release.** He flicks it forward. A violet flash, a huge deep blow; the camera rides behind the sphere,
  in the tunnel it is cutting, the world tearing past; then cuts high over him, looking down the whole length of its
  path as the trench opens behind it.
- **What it does.** The sphere flies straight along where he looked, **3.5 blocks a tick** (70 a second), for
  **200 blocks** (`hollowPurpleRange`), then collapses in a flash.
  - **Blocks are erased**: no drops, no neighbour updates, nothing left of what a chest held, a clean round tunnel
    of `hollowPurpleRadius` through hills, forests, houses and water. Not what cannot be broken: bedrock, the end portal
    frame, barriers, command blocks (anything of negative hardness). Plants and torches on the rim of the cut, which
    would hang in the air, are taken too. With `hollowPurpleErases` off it kills but leaves the world alone.
  - **Everything living in it dies**, players too (as in the show), but not players in creative or spectator, and not
    him. **Bosses** (the `c:bosses` tag: the Wither, the Ender Dragon) are not killed: they take 150 damage once per
    cast, which is a very heavy blow and not the end of them. Whatever else is in it (items, experience, arrows,
    paintings) is simply erased.
  - It is done **a slice a tick, behind the sphere**, within a budget of about 60 000 blocks looked at a tick (the
    slice is cut shorter to fit), so it does not lag: a 200-block run through a mountain at radius 3 is 1 to 3 ms a
    tick, and a radius of 16 stays inside the budget.
- **Fits the other rules:** not cast while a Genesis Key event is going on; not while he is in his Domain (nor a Domain
  while a Purple is going: "Already casting"); the cooldown (**5 minutes**, `hollowPurpleCooldownSeconds`) shows on the
  blindfold as the Domain's does (one overlay for both, whichever is further from ready, and each power keeps its own
  time). It is cut short, with nothing erased, if he dies or leaves before the release.
- **Sound** is all made by `tools/gen_gojolander_sounds.py`, none of it recorded (`purple_blue`, `purple_red`,
  `purple_merge`, `purple_release`, `purple_roar`, `purple_fade`, each with a mono ".world" version for everyone who is
  not the caster). The deep parts carry their own overtones so a phone speaker plays them. **His voice line** has a
  slot, `purple.voice` (played as the orbs meet, for the caster and for those in reach): it is empty. To fill it,
  put a clip at `gojolander/src/main/resources/assets/gojolander/sounds/purple_voice.ogg` and add a `purple.voice`
  entry to `sounds.json` naming `gojolander:purple_voice`.
- **Works with Sodium and Iris**, like the Domain: it is drawn in the same way (shootingstar's `WorldPasses`).

### Game rules for Hollow Purple

| Rule | Default | Range | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| `hollowPurpleRange` | 200 | 10 to 1000 | How far the sphere flies from where it is released, in blocks. |
| `hollowPurpleRadius` | 3 | 1 to 16 | The radius of the tunnel (and of the sphere), in blocks. |
| `hollowPurpleErases` | true | | Whether it erases blocks. Off, it still kills what is in its way. |
| `hollowPurpleCooldownSeconds` | 300 | 0 to 7200 | How long before he can cast it again. |

`/gojolander purple <player>` casts it as if they had pressed the key (the same rules hold); `/gojolander ready
<player>` makes both of their powers ready.

## How it is made

- `gojolander/` is its own Gradle subproject and jar (`gojolander-<version>.jar`), set up like `chitty/`. Its tests
  (`DomainGameTests`) and its client self test (`DomainSelfTest`, `-Pselftest=domain`, screenshots of it cast, watched
  from outside and caught in) are in the root project's harness, and so is its multiplayer test (`tools/mptest.sh
  domain`: a dedicated server and three clients, one casting it, one caught, one looking on from outside; twice, the
  second time the caster leaving the game in the middle of it; `DomainMultiplayerServer` logs a PASS or FAIL for
  each thing it checks).
- A film of it for a Short, from his own eyes (`FilmDomain`, `python tools/film.py domain`): Kortev comes at him with
  a sword, is caught, and is finished off after it fades. Cut with `python tools/shorts.py tools/shorts/domain.json`.
  His skin there is a stand-in (`tools/film_skin.py`) until there is the friend's own: put it at
  build/selftest/skins/Rival.png.
- The server: `domain/DomainManager` (who may cast, catching, holding, the barrier, letting go), against
  `domain/DomainTimeline`. The client: `ClientDomains` (state, sounds), `DomainRender` with `gl_domain.fsh` (the ink,
  the black rising, the sphere, the black and the reveal, the void, the shattering, in one full-screen pass) and
  `gl_rim.fsh` (whoever is in the void with you, lit by the eye), `gl_sky.fsh` (the void's smooth sky, painted
  into a small panorama every other frame so the pass only looks it up: the pass is about a fifth of what it was), `DomainHud` with `gl_flood.fsh` (the flood, the
  name), `DomainCamera` (his shots and the punch-in), `BlindfoldFeature`, `HandSign`, `PlayerModelMixin` and
  `HeldTrembleMixin` (the cloth, the eyes, his hands lifting and lowering it, the sign, the caught trembling);
  `sight/SixEyes` and `SixEyesClient` with `SixEyesMixin` and `SixEyesColorMixin` (the Six Eyes, drawn as the
  game's own outlines). The self
  test logs the frame rate and each pass's cost once a second (`[perf]` in the log).
- Hollow Purple: server `purple/PurpleManager` (casting, the carving slice by slice, what dies) against
  `purple/PurpleTimeline`; client `ClientPurples` (state, sounds, dust), `PurpleRender` with `gl_purple.fsh` (Blue, Red,
  the merge and the sphere, in one full-screen pass), `PurpleCamera` (its shots), `PurplePose` (his arms, and where his
  hands are for the orbs), `PurpleHud` (the flash, the name). Its tests are `PurpleGameTests`; its film is `FilmPurple`
  (`bash work/tools/film_purple.sh`: a 12 to 15 s tall test clip, the friend on a hill, a forest, a village and a
  mountain before him). Its name's brushed texture: `python tools/gen_gojolander_textures.py purple`; its sounds are
  made with the others (`python tools/gen_gojolander_sounds.py purple_blue ...`: name them, so the old ones stay as they are).
- Sounds: `python tools/gen_gojolander_sounds.py` (all of them in one run: each run builds its reverbs from its first
  sound, so one made alone comes out a little different); textures: `python tools/gen_gojolander_textures.py`. The deep
  sounds carry their own overtones so a phone's or laptop's speaker still plays them.
